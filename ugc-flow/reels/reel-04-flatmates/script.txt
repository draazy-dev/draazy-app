====================================================

PAIN ............... Three pains, in order: (1) endless scrolling through Telegram/Facebook/WhatsApp flatmate
                     groups with nothing useful; (2) the people you do find are total strangers, nothing verified;
                     (3) you only learn after moving in that your habits don't match.
DRAAZY TRUTH ....... Flatmates "vibe check": 12 quick taps on living habits (tidy, food, sleep, WFH, guests,

                     flatmates.json "12 quick taps · match % on every post"). People can carry a "KYC done" badge
                     (optional, so "KYC-done log dikhte hain", never "sab verified"). Never name Telegram/Facebook/
                     WhatsApp in a video prompt (Flow policy, lesson 261); only in the local overlay and caption.
