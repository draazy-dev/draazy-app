REEL 06 - RENT AGREEMENT  ("Sab online... phir bhi agent ki fees?")
===================================================================

PAIN ............... The rent agreement is all online now, yet the agent still takes a fat fee. On top of that comes an extra
                     trip for biometrics, and you have to remember the rent date and the renewal yourself.
DRAAZY TRUTH ....... Draazy prepares the Leave & Licence agreement and handles e-registration, and biometrics happen at your
                     doorstep. Two kinds of reminder arrive on their own: a monthly rent-pay reminder to the tenant, and a
                     reminder to renew the agreement before it expires. Renewal is one tap, prefilled from the last
                     agreement.
                     NEVER say "auto-renew" or "auto renewal": every renewal needs fresh registration and biometrics.
                     NO PRICES anywhere (the user's call): no agent fee figure, no Draazy price.
                     Never say "registration office" or "line": nobody queues for this any more.
CAST ............... Ishaan, the indoor character (teal pullover hoodie), on the sofa of the 1BHK he has just moved into
DESIGN ............. one set, one time of day, one outfit, one framing (00-CONTINUITY-RULES.txt R1).
                     The tease and the spoken sign-off are inside the last clip; there is no separate sign-off clip.
ORDER .............. set.txt (0 cr) -> clip-1 -> user approves -> save r06-anchor -> clip-2, clip-3 -> download 720p
CREDITS ............ 36 base (3 x 12, native 720p, user-approved); one retake (12) and one continuity edit (10) in reserve
LENGTH ............. about 25 s = 3 clips trimmed to about 7.7 s, plus the HTML end card (about 2 s). Trim points come from
                     the audio envelope.
