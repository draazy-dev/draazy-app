REEL 03 - FAKE OWNER  ("Owner asli tha... ya nahi?")
====================================================

PAIN ............... A so-called owner shows the flat, promises the agreement tomorrow, then goes silent. He was never the
                     owner. You can't tell a real owner from a fake one before you commit.
DRAAZY TRUTH ....... Two separate checks, both done by a Draazy reviewer (frontend home FAQ, OwnershipEvidenceTypes):
                     1. KYC done: the owner photographs a government ID (Aadhaar, PAN or driving licence) with a live
                        selfie, and a reviewer decides.
                     2. Verified property: the owner uploads ownership papers (Index II, 7/12, property card or share
                        certificate; for rentals also a tax receipt or electricity bill), and a reviewer checks them.
                     Never claim: photos checked against the real flat, "100% genuine", guaranteed title, or that
                     Draazy stops every scam. The badges mean a reviewer checked the ID and sighted the papers.

                     The tease and the spoken sign-off are inside the last clip; there is no separate sign-off clip.

LENGTH ............. about 25 s = 3 clips trimmed to about 7.7 s, plus the HTML end card (about 2 s). Trim points come from
                     the audio envelope.
