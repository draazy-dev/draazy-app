CHARACTER 1 (LEAD): ISHAAN
=========================

WHO HE IS
  Ishaan, 23. Software engineer at an IT park in Hinjawadi, Pune. Moved from Indore 8 months ago.
  Lives in a small rented room in Wakad. Has hunted for a flat three times and has a story for every
  broker. Dry, deadpan humour, never loud. Talks to the camera like texting a close friend.
  Relatable, a little tired, always fair. Never mocks anyone personally; he laughs at the situation.

  short black hair that is textured and slightly messy on top with a low taper fade on the sides,
  neat light stubble, thick dark expressive eyebrows, dark brown eyes, clear even skin with no moles or spots, lean build, about 175 cm tall. He wears his signature teal (#14B8A6) oversized

  digital watch on his left wrist. His phone has a matte teal case.

  over the same white t-shirt, black joggers, white sneakers, black backpack. Swap only the clothing
  sentence; never change the face description.

VOICE - LOCKED (paste into every prompt where he speaks)
  Ishaan speaks in casual Hinglish with a natural Indian accent: a youthful early-20s voice,
  clearly male and mid-pitched, never high or older-sounding, relaxed and conversational, a quick pace with small deadpan pauses,
  close to the microphone like a phone's front-camera recording.

PERSONALITY ON CAMERA
  Deadpan stare at the lens after a punchline. Small eye-roll. Leans in and lowers his voice for the
  "socho..." line, as if sharing a secret. A half-smile on the sign-off "Direct hai, easy hai, Draazy hai. Bahut jald launch ho raha hai."
