# voice.md

Read by every `ig-*` skill. Brand account, not a person.

## Who we are

- **Name:** Draazy
- **Handle:** @draazyapp.homes
- **What we do:** Pune's broker-free property marketplace: rent, buy or sell straight from owners, find a flatmate, and get a rent agreement done at your doorstep.
- **Who we talk to:** People in Pune hunting for a flat or a flatmate, mostly 21-35, many new to the city for a job (Hinjewadi, Kharadi, Magarpatta, Baner, Wakad, Viman Nagar). Second audience: owners who want to list without brokers.
- **What we sell:** Owner listings with zero brokerage, flatmate matching, rent agreements (drafting, stamp duty, doorstep biometrics, renewal reminder and one-tap renewal).

## What we sound like

- **Tone:** a sharp Pune friend who has been through the flat hunt. Dry about the pain, never sad or whiny, upbeat about the fix.
- **Our own lines:** "Not on Draazy." / "You talk straight to the owner." / "Your number stays private until you choose to share it." / "One app. For all of it."
- **Words we use:** owner, flatmate, zero brokerage, straight to, your number, locality names, commute.
- **Words we never use:** game-changer, revolutionary, seamless, hassle-free, unlock, elevate, "stop scrolling", "follow for more".
- **Swearing:** no.
- **Emoji in captions:** never in the hook; at most one elsewhere.
- **Face on camera:** motion reels are faceless (voiceover); UGC reels use the recurring characters in `ugc-flow\`.
- **Voiceover:** en-IN female (Neerja Expressive) at 1.08x for motion reels; Hinglish allowed in UGC reels.
- **Pace:** about 175 wpm.

## Our positions

1. You should never pay someone to open a door the owner already listed.
2. Your phone number is yours; sharing it should be your choice, not the price of looking at a flat.
3. The flatmate matters more than the flat.
4. Search by your commute, not by the locality name.
5. A rent agreement should not cost you a day at the registration office.

## Off limits

- **No prices or fee figures**, ours or anyone's (no broker fee amounts, no Draazy pricing, no rupee figures).
- **Verification claims:** only "KYC done" (owners) and "Verified property" (listings). Never "verified owner", "100% genuine" or similar.
- **Renewal:** "renewal reminder" and "one-tap renewal". Never "auto-renew".
- **No app screens** in motion reels.
- **No money-scam stories** in dialogue (blocked by video tools and off-brand).
