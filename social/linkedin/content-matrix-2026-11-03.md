## Draft: Building Draazy in public × Observation (published 2026-11-03)

We've spent the last few weeks reading Pune rental threads before writing more code.

Not surveys. Real posts from people hunting flats in Hinjewadi, Baner, Kharadi and Wakad.

Five things kept coming up:

1. Brokers posting as owners. People don't mind paying for real help. They mind paying for a forwarded phone number.
2. Deposit fights at move-out, mostly because nobody took photos at move-in.
3. Token money paid before anyone checked the papers.
4. Society rules you only hear about after shifting in: bachelors, pets, guests.
5. Water. In some areas it decides everything, and listings never mention it.

None of these are listing problems. They're trust problems.

That's what we're building Draazy around: you talk straight to the owner, and your number stays private until you choose to share it.

We're not live yet. If you've rented in Pune, which of these hit you hardest? Or did we miss one?
