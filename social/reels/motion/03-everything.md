## Angle

- **Topic:** positioning. Draazy is the whole property app (rent, buy, sell, flatmates, agreements), not just rentals.
- **Draazy truth:**
  - real owners, zero brokerage
  - commute search
  - flatmate matching
  - doorstep-registered rent agreement
- **Audience:** anyone moving home in Pune; a good profile-pinned reel.

## Hook

"Rent. Buy. Sell. Flatmates. Rent agreements." Five numbered panels, 01–05, wipe in one per word.
