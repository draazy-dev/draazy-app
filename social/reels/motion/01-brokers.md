## Angle

- **Pain:** one search, and every broker in the city has your number.
- **Draazy truth:**
  - talk straight to the owner
  - zero brokerage, zero middlemen
  - "KYC done" and "Verified property" badges
  - your number stays private until you share it
- **Audience:** renters; works as a cold top-of-funnel ad.

## Hook

"You search for one flat in Pune, and forty-seven brokers call you back."

On screen, "One flat. in Pune" with a pin, then a broker-call counter racing up.
