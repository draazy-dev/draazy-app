## Angle

- **Pain:** the flat that "looked close" still takes an hour in Hinjawadi traffic.
- **Draazy truth:** search by commute time from your office, then check the society and the drive before visiting.
- **Audience:** IT professionals (Hinjawadi, Wakad, Kharadi).

## Hook

"Your office is in Hinjawadi. But the flat that looked close can still take an hour in traffic."
