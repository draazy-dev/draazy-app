## Angle

- **Pain:** you share your number for one flat, and by evening ten brokers are calling.
- **Draazy truth:**
  - your number stays masked
  - the owner's number is hidden until they approve
  - no reply in 7 days, and your contact comes back
  - chat happens in-app
  - Draazy never sells your number to brokers
- **Audience:** renters who have been spammed before.

## Hook
