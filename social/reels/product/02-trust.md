## Angle

- **Pain:** an "owner" wants a token advance before you've even seen the flat.
- **Draazy truth:**
  - "KYC done" means a government ID plus a live selfie, approved by a person and not a bot.
  - "Verified property" means an ownership paper (electricity bill, Index II, tax receipt, share certificate, 7/12) was checked. It's a document check, not a registry search.
- **Audience:** renters worried about scams.

## Hook

"Found the perfect flat in Pune? But the owner wants a token before you've even seen it?"
