## Angle

- **Pain:** renewing the rent agreement means another day of running around.
- **Draazy truth:**
  - fill in your details once, and the owner joins from a link
  - Draazy drafts it, handles stamp duty, and registers it at your doorstep
  - both sides get a renewal reminder two months before it ends
  - renewal is one prefilled tap
  - there's a monthly rent reminder
- **Audience:** tenants already renting, plus owners.

## Hook

"Renewing your rent agreement again? That means one more day at the registration office."
