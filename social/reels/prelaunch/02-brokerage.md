## Angle

- **Pain:** a broker opens a door, flicks a light on, and asks for a month's rent as brokerage.
- **Draazy truth (teased):** deal with the owner directly, zero brokerage.
- **Audience:** renters who have paid brokerage before.
- **Positioning:** "Pune ka apna rental app" (pre-pivot).

## Hook

- On screen (0–2.5 s): **5-minute visit. ₹25,000 fee.** ("₹25,000" in coral)
- The ₹25,000 is the skit's number, not a statistic. It was made before the "no prices in reels" rule; future reels must not show ₹ figures.
