## Angle

- **Pain:** a so-called owner shows the flat, promises the agreement "tomorrow", then goes silent. He was never the owner.
- **Draazy truth (teased):** two separate checks, both by a Draazy reviewer:
  - **KYC done:** the owner's government ID plus a live selfie.
  - **Verified property:** ownership papers such as Index II, 7/12, property card, share certificate, tax receipt or electricity bill.
- **Never claim:** that photos are checked against the flat, "100% genuine", guaranteed title, or that Draazy stops every scam.
- **Positioning:** whole property app, so the sign-off is "Direct hai, easy hai, Draazy hai."

## Hook

- On screen (0–2.5 s): **Owner asli tha... ya nahi?** ("asli" in coral)
