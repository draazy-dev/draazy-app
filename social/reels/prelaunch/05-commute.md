## Angle

- **Pain:** no flat near the office, so you rent a cheap one far away, then lose 3 hours a day and the savings to traffic and cabs.
- **Draazy truth (teased):** search around your office by distance or travel time; each property shows drive times to the IT hubs.
- **Audience:** IT professionals choosing between rent and commute (Kharadi, Hinjawadi).

## Hook

- On screen (0–2.5 s): **Cheap rent. 90-minute commute.** ("90-minute" in coral)
- The 90 minutes is the skit's case, not a statistic: Wagholi to Kharadi is about 10 km, but Nagar Road crawls at peak hour.
