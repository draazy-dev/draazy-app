## Angle

- **Pain, in order:**
  1. weeks of scrolling flatmate groups with nothing useful
  2. the people you find are total strangers, with nothing verified
  3. you only learn after moving in that your habits don't match
- **Draazy truth (teased):**
  - the flatmates "vibe check": 12 quick taps on living habits give a match % on every post
  - people can carry a "KYC done" badge. It's optional, so say "KYC-done log dikhte hain", never "sab verified".
- **Audience:** flatmate seekers (campaign keyword FLATMATE).

## Hook

- On screen (0–2.5 s): **10 groups. Zero flatmates.** ("Zero" in coral)
