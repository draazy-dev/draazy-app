## Angle

- **Pain:**
  - the agreement is all online, yet the agent still takes a fat fee
  - there's an extra trip for biometrics
  - you have to remember the rent date and the renewal yourself
- **Draazy truth (teased):**
  - Draazy prepares the Leave & Licence agreement and handles e-registration
  - biometrics happen at your doorstep
  - a monthly rent reminder, plus a renewal reminder before expiry
  - renewal is one tap, prefilled from the last agreement
- **Never say:**
  - "auto-renew": every renewal needs fresh registration
  - any price
  - "registration office" or "line": nobody queues any more

## Hook

- On screen (0–2.5 s): **Sab online... phir bhi agent ki fees?** ("fees" in coral)
