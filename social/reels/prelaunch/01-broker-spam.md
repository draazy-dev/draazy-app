## Angle

- **Pain:** you enquire about one flat and within hours a dozen brokers have your number, and you're in WhatsApp groups you never joined.
- **Draazy truth (teased, not shown):** you reach the owner directly, your number stays masked, and Draazy doesn't sell numbers to brokers.
- **Audience:** renters in their first Pune flat hunt.
- **Positioning:** "Pune ka apna rental app" (made before the whole-property-app pivot; reels 3–7 use the newer tagline).

## Hook

- On screen (0–2.5 s): **1 enquiry. 12 broker calls.** ("calls" in coral)
- First words: "Kal maine sirf ek flat pe enquiry ki thi, bas ek."
