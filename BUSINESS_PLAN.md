# Draazy — Business Plan & Feature Strategy
### Zero-brokerage real estate platform | Aggressive customer-acquisition playbook for India
*Prepared as a founder-level strategy document. Built on top of the existing Draazy web prototype.*

---

## 0. The one-line thesis

> **Don't fight NoBroker and MagicBricks nationally. Win Pune so completely that you own it — then clone the playbook city by city.**

NoBroker (~$1B+ valuation) and MagicBricks/Housing.com have huge war chests. You cannot out-spend them across 50 cities. You *can* out-execute them in **one city** with deeper hyperlocal supply, better trust, and a tighter community loop. Every category-defining Indian consumer company (Swiggy in Bangalore, Meesho in resellers, NoBroker itself in Bangalore rentals) started by dominating a wedge before going wide.

## 2. Competitive teardown — where the giants are weak

| Player | Model | Strength | Exploitable weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NoBroker** | Zero-brokerage + paid plans + services + NoBrokerHood SaaS | Brand = "no broker", services flywheel, society SaaS moat | Aggressive paywalls frustrate users (contact limits); support complaints; spreads thin across cities — **shallow in any single locality** |
| **MagicBricks / 99acres** | Classifieds, builder ads | Massive listing inventory, SEO | Broker-heavy listings = **fake/stale listings, spam calls**; no trust layer; not owner-direct |
| **Housing.com** | Classifieds + content | UX, brand, new-project focus | Same broker-spam problem; metro-Tier-1 focus |
| **Offline brokers** | 1–2 months' rent commission | Hyperlocal trust, hand-holding | Expensive, opaque, no tech, no documentation |

### Your three structural wedges
1. **Trust as a product, not a tagline.** Verified owners (document + selfie, staff-reviewed — *already built*), owner phone-privacy with request/approve (*already built*), and a closed-deal "maker-checker" finalize flow (*already built*) directly attack the #1 complaint: **fake listings + spam calls**.
2. **Hyperlocal density.** One society, one locality at a time → highest listing-to-seeker liquidity in Pune. Density beats breadth in two-sided marketplaces.
3. **Full-stack convenience.** You already have rent agreements, rent receipts (HRA), document vault, finances. Bundle the *entire* move-in/own journey, capturing services revenue the classifieds can't.

## 4. The aggressive customer-acquisition playbook

> Two-sided marketplaces die from the **cold-start problem**. Solve **supply (owners/listings) first** — seekers always follow listings. Below is a sequenced, aggressive plan.

### 4.1 Solve supply first (Months 0–3): "Liquidity in 30 localities"
- **Free everything for owners, forever, on the core.** Free listing, free verified badge, free rent agreement draft, free tenant verification. Make listing on Draazy a no-brainer vs. paying a broker.
- **Field "supply squads."** 4–6 hyperlocal reps physically onboard owners in target localities (Wakad, Hinjewadi, Baner, Kharadi, Viman Nagar, Kothrud). Goal: **80%+ listing density** in 20–30 societies before any demand spend. *(This is how NoBroker actually bootstrapped — feet on the street, not just ads.)*
- **Broker-free guarantee + "report a broker" bounty** to keep listings clean.

### 4.2 Society / RWA land-grab (Months 1–6): the NoBrokerHood move
- Offer **free society-management software** (visitor/gate management, maintenance dues, notices, complaint tracking) to apartment RWAs.
- This is the **highest-leverage acquisition channel in Indian real estate**: each society = hundreds of pre-verified households, recurring owner *and* tenant supply, and intra-society resale/rental demand. It's also your **stickiest moat** (recurring SaaS, switching cost).
- Target: **50 societies in 6 months**. Every society becomes a self-refilling supply+demand pool.

### 4.3 Demand ignition (Months 3–9)
- **WhatsApp-first distribution.** India is WhatsApp-native. Auto-share listings, visit confirmations, and price-drop alerts via WhatsApp. Click-to-WhatsApp ads convert far cheaper than web forms.
- **Hyperlocal creator content.** Sponsor Pune Instagram/YouTube creators for society walk-through reels (video-first discovery is the 2026 trend). 10–20 micro-creators >> one big billboard.
- **High-intent performance marketing.** Bid on *"2BHK rent Wakad no brokerage"*, *"flats in Baner without broker"*. Long-tail, hyperlocal, cheap, high-conversion.
- **Local SEO moat.** Programmatic locality + society landing pages ("Rent in <Society>, <Locality>") — your descriptive H1s already do this.

### 4.4 Viral & referral loops (always on)
- **Two-sided cash referral.** ₹X to referrer + ₹X to referee on a closed deal. Both owner→owner and tenant→tenant.
- **"Move-in money-back guarantee."** If a verified listing turns out fake, refund + ₹ credit. Converts trust skeptics; near-zero payout if verification works.
- **Free rent agreement as a hook.** People search "rent agreement Pune" constantly. Give it free → capture the lead → upsell registration, tenant verification, packers.
- **HRA rent-receipt generator** (already built) — viral every Jan–Mar tax season; pure top-of-funnel.

### 4.5 The "unfair" aggressive bets (pick 1–2, fund heavily)
- **Zero-brokerage + we-pay-your-first-broker-bill switch offer** for users mid-deal with a broker.
- **Deposit-free / deposit-financing renting** (fintech): tenants pay deposit in EMIs; massive conversion unlock for young renters.
- **"List in 5 minutes or we do it for you"** — concierge listing via WhatsApp photos.

## 5. Monetisation — how Draazy makes money

> Strictly broker-free. Keep the **core free and generous**, charge small one-off amounts only where the user is clearly better off, and **never auto-renew anything**. MVP target: ~₹10,000/month to cover infrastructure, run by a team of 2–4 with no assisted (relationship-manager) plans.

**MVP revenue — the only three paid items (decided 2026-10-11):**

| # | Product | Price | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Rent agreement** (first or renewal) | ₹499 + government charges | Core driver. Sold to anyone in Pune, not only to deals found on Draazy. |
| 2 | **Listing pass** | ₹999 | Each extra *live* rent/sell listing beyond the 1 free one; 60 days. |
| 3 | **Contact pack** | ₹199 | 15 owner contacts, valid 30 days, for buyers and tenants. |

All prices include GST; government charges are stamp duty and registration, passed through at cost.

**Free, on purpose:** 1 live listing at a time, unlimited flatmate posts, 15 owner contacts in the first 30 days, referral rewards, verified badge, 7 days of Featured placement after ownership verification, rent receipts, document vault, renewal reminders.

**Commission-only partners (no price shown to users):** registration lawyer, home-loan agent.

**Owner retention loop:** 60 days before an agreement ends, owner and tenant get a renewal prompt (one tap, prefilled, ₹499) or a "tenant moving out → list it again from the end date" prompt; a shareable "brokerage saved" card follows every registration.

**Later, only once a locality has real liquidity:** services marketplace commissions, Society SaaS, pay-rent and deposit financing. Never: broker plans, broker listings or paid broker leads.

**Unit-economics principle:** A pure listing earns paise; a *move-in* (agreement + verification + movers + loan + rent payments) earns thousands. **Optimise for completed move-ins, not page views.** Your "finalize the deal" flow is the instrumentation point — every finalised deal should trigger a services upsell (you already route to `services.html` on close).

## 6. Feature roadmap — what to put in the app (and why)

> ✅ = already in your prototype · 🔨 = build next · 🚀 = growth/scale

### Phase 1 — Trust & liquidity MVP (now → 3 months)
- ✅ Zero-brokerage owner-direct listings
- ✅ Identity / owner verification + "Verified" badge (lean into this hard in UI — it's the differentiator)
- ✅ Owner phone privacy + request/approve contact (kills spam-call complaint)
- ✅ Listing post flow, dashboard, saved/recent, scheduled visits
- ✅ Free rent agreement generation + HRA rent receipts (acquisition hooks)
- 🔨 **Map-based + locality/society search** with commute-time filter ("near my office")
- 🔨 **WhatsApp integration** — share listing, get alerts, confirm visits, OTP login
- 🔨 **Real backend** (replace localStorage): auth (OTP), listings DB, search, media storage. *This is the critical engineering unblock — prototype is front-end only.*
- 🔨 **Fraud/quality layer:** duplicate-listing detection, owner re-verification, "report listing"

### Phase 2 — Monetise the journey (3–9 months)
- 🔨 **Services marketplace** (packers, painting, cleaning, legal) with commission tracking
- 🔨 **Tenant verification + police verification** as paid add-on
- 🔨 **Rent agreement e-stamp + registration** (paid), doorstep delivery
- 🔨 **Home-loan & insurance** lead-gen (bank/NBFC partners) — your finances module is the natural home
- 🔨 **Pay-rent-online** + deposit financing (fintech partner)
- ✅ **Pay-per-use** — listing pass and contact pack (no subscriptions, no auto-renewal)
- 🔨 **Draazy Society** — visitor/gate/maintenance SaaS (the recurring moat)
- 🔨 **Ratings & reviews** for societies, owners, localities (trust UGC)

### Phase 3 — Scale & defend (9–18 months)
- 🚀 **AI features:** price estimator/valuation, listing auto-fill from photos, AI search ("3BHK under 30k near Hinjewadi, pet-friendly"), fraud scoring, recommendation feed
- 🚀 **Video-first listings:** mandatory video walkthroughs, virtual tours, creator reels feed
- 🚀 **Builder/new-launch marketplace** (high-ticket lead-gen)
- 🚀 **Resale & home-loan-to-registration full-stack buy journey**
- 🚀 **City-2 launch** (e.g., Pimpri-Chinchwad adjacency, then Nashik/Nagpur or a metro) — repeat the playbook with the proven runbook

### Highest ROI features to prioritise (do these first)
1. **Real backend + OTP/WhatsApp login** (unblocks everything)
2. **Map + commute search** (table-stakes for seekers; you're missing it)
3. **Society SaaS** (best acquisition + stickiest revenue)
4. **Services marketplace** (where the margin is)
5. **Aggressive verification UI** (your trust differentiator — make it loud)

## 8. Financial logic (illustrative, not a forecast)

- **CAC strategy:** Supply CAC near-zero (free + field + society virality). Demand CAC kept low via WhatsApp/long-tail SEO/referrals vs. expensive brand ads.
- **LTV drivers:** A single move-in can monetise across agreement + verification + movers + loan + rent payments + (if society) recurring SaaS. **Target LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1** within 12 months by stacking services on each move-in.
- **Burn discipline:** Because you're single-city, you can reach **contribution-positive in Pune** while NoBroker subsidises 50 cities. **Profitable density beats unprofitable breadth** — and is the story that raises your next round.
- **Funding ask framing:** "We are the #1 zero-brokerage platform in Pune with X% locality density and positive unit economics; we're raising to clone the runbook to N cities."

## 9. Risks & how you neutralise them

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Cold-start (two-sided)** | Supply-first + society land-grab; never spend on demand before liquidity exists in a locality |
| **Giants outspend you** | Don't match spend; win density + trust in one city; be capital-efficient and profitable |
| **Fake listings / spam (category-killer)** | Lean into verification, phone-privacy, maker-checker finalize, report-bounty — make trust the product |
| **Thin listing margins** | Monetise services & transactions, not listings |
| **Regulatory (RERA, data, e-stamp)** | RERA-compliant builder listings; partner for e-stamp/registration; identity documents handled per DPDP Act |
| **Society SaaS competition (NoBrokerHood, MyGate)** | Bundle free with property liquidity they can't match locally; Pune-first service depth |
| **Trust to transact online** | Money-back guarantee, escrow for deposits, verified reviews |
