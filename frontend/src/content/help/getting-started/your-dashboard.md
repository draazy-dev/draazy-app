Your dashboard is where everything you have started on Draazy comes together. Open it from your profile menu after you sign in. On a phone it appears as a bottom navigation; on a desktop it is a sidebar. Which tabs you see depends on whether you are a seeker, a tenant or an owner. A red count on a tab means something is waiting for you.

## Home

**Needs your attention** is your action center. It groups what is waiting for you, shows how many are pending and how many days the oldest has been waiting, and takes you to the right tab. When there is nothing to do it says **You're all caught up**.

Below it are four tiles:

- Owners: **Waiting on you**, **Visits to confirm**, **Live**, **Views**
- Seekers: **Saved**, **Saved searches**, **Upcoming visits**, **Following**

Home also shows one suggestion at a time, in this order: **New matches for your alerts**, **Verification in review**, **Get your KYC badge** (tap **Verify**), or **Complete your profile**. If you were searching, **Pick up where you left off** has a **Resume search** button. Seekers may also see **Recently viewed** or **Recommended**.

## Requests

Everything that needs a yes or no from another person lives here. Use the sub-tabs to filter: **All**, **Number requests**, **Photo requests**, **Documents**, **Flatmate**, **Visits** and **Offers**.

Requests you receive have buttons such as **Approve & chat**, **Confirm & chat**, **Accept & chat**, **Accept**, **Share**, **Decline**, **Chat first**, **Add photos** and **Counter**. You can also keep a private note on a request. Requests you send show **Remind** (with a short wait between reminders) and **Withdraw**. A withdrawn contact request returns your contact credit, and you can ask the same owner again after 7 days.

Approving a contact request shares your number and opens a chat. See [Messages and chat](/help/a/messages-and-chat) and [Managing leads](/help/a/managing-leads).

## Visits

Your visit requests and bookings, with their state: **Awaiting confirmation**, **Confirmed**, **Visited**, **Cancelled** or **No-show**. See [Scheduling a visit](/help/a/schedule-a-visit).

## My Properties (owners)

Your listings and flatmate posts in one list. Filter by **All types**, **Properties**, **Flatmate rooms**, **Flatmate requests** or **Flatmate groups**. The **Rent-O-Meter** helps you check your rent against the area. See [Posting a listing](/help/a/post-a-listing) and [Listing freshness](/help/a/listing-freshness).

## Rental (tenants)

**The home you rent** shows your landlord, your rent agreement and your Verified-Tenant score ("Trust score"). Tiles on the page:

- **Rent agreement**: **Create & e-register**, or **Registered · view** once done. If it is incomplete, an **Action needed: complete your rent agreement** banner takes you to it. See [Rent agreement basics](/help/a/rent-agreement-basics).
- **Rent record**: track the rent you already pay.
- **Pay rent**: marked as coming soon. Draazy does not collect your rent today.

When your lease is close to ending, a card shows **Your lease ends on** the date, with options to renew, find your next home or book movers.

## Finances

- Owners: **Activity**, **Insights** and **Reports** for your rental income.
- Tenants: your Rent Passport, HRA, deposit and EMI information.

## Documents

Your private **Document Vault**, in three groups: **Property docs**, **My Tenancy** and **Personal**. When a buyer asks for a document, you choose **Grant** or **Decline**. This is also where your **Rent Agreement** and HRA rent receipts are kept, and where an owner can request the **Verified property** badge. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it. See [Your data and privacy](/help/a/your-data-privacy).

## Alerts & activity

- **Alerts**: **Property & Flatmate Alerts**, with a **New alert** button, plus **Followed Societies**
- **Recently Viewed**
- **Flatmate groups**

See [Saved searches and alerts](/help/a/saved-searches-alerts).

## Plan & Billing

**What you have** lists your balances: **Owner contacts left**, **Free listing slots left**, **Listing passes active** and **Referral rewards left**. **See prices** opens the live [Plans page](/plans). **Payment history** lists each payment as **Paid**, **Refund pending**, **Refunded**, **Refund declined** or **Disputed**, and **Request a refund** opens a request that is reviewed within 2 business days. See [Plans explained](/help/a/plans-explained) and [Invoices, refunds and receipts](/help/a/invoices-and-refunds).

## Profile

Your name, email, city, privacy and notification settings, and the **KYC done badge** card with **Verify identity**. See [Managing your profile](/help/a/manage-profile).
