You can browse Draazy without an account. You need one to contact owners, save properties, schedule visits or list a property.

## Signing up

1. Open **Sign up** from the public site.
2. Enter your **full name**, optional email and **10-digit Indian mobile number**. This number is your login identity. If a friend invited you, use their link or sign up with the number they invited. No code is needed. If you have a referral code, you can still enter it in the optional **Referral code** field.
3. Accept the terms and tap **Send OTP**.
4. Enter the 6-digit OTP we send to your number on WhatsApp to finish creating the account.

### When a sheet asks you to sign in

If you tap a gated action while signed out (contact an owner, save a home, book a visit, list a property), a sign-in sheet opens over the page instead of taking you away. Enter your number and the OTP, and the action carries on where you left it. If the number is new, the sheet asks for your name (and optional email) first.

### If the code does not work

A code is valid for **5 minutes** and you get **3 guesses**. After a wrong code the form tells you how many are left, and once they are used up you need to tap **Resend OTP** for a new code. Switching to WhatsApp and back is fine; the 5 minutes run on the clock, not on how long the tab was in front.

### Not receiving the OTP?

- Wait for the **Resend in Ns** countdown to finish before requesting another code. Repeated requests can rate-limit your number, and the screen then says how long to wait.
- Check that the number is active on WhatsApp and can receive messages from businesses. We send OTPs on WhatsApp, not by SMS.
- If it still fails after two attempts, contact support with your number and the approximate time you tried.

> [!TIP]
> Use the same number you will answer calls on. It is the number an owner sees once they approve your contact request, and a number you do not pick up is the single most common reason enquiries go cold.

## Signing in later

Sign-in uses the same OTP flow, so there is no password to forget. **Remember this device** is selected by default and keeps you signed in after you close the browser. Turn it off on a shared device and sign out when you finish.

Your number is your login, so it cannot be changed from the app. If you need to move an account to a new number, message Draazy support; they will tell you what proof is needed.

## After you sign in

Your [dashboard](/help/a/your-dashboard) is the home for requests, visits, billing and documents, and [Messages](/help/a/messages-and-chat) holds your chats.
