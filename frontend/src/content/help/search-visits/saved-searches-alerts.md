In a tight locality, the good listings are taken within 48 hours. Saved searches exist so you are not refreshing the page.

## Saving a search

1. Apply your filters on [Listings](/listings).
2. Tap **Save search** (the bell) beside the smart-search bar above the results. You will be asked to sign in first if you have not.
3. That is it — the search is saved straight away, named after its filters (or after the text you typed in smart search), and starts on **Daily**.
4. Change the alert frequency any time from your dashboard.

If a search finds nothing, or fewer than three homes, the page also offers **Create alert** so you hear about new matches.

## Alert frequency

| Frequency | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| Instant | Hot localities, moving in under two weeks |
| Daily | Normal search, 1–2 months of runway |
| Weekly | Browsing, no fixed timeline |
| Off | Keep the search saved without notifications |

Alerts arrive as in-app notifications, and only when new matches have appeared. Daily and Weekly mean at most one alert a day or a week; Instant means the next check, within about half an hour of a home being listed. You can switch match alerts off or set quiet hours in [Notifications and appearance](/help/a/notifications-language).

> [!TIP]
> Run two saved searches: one narrow and instant, one broader and daily. The narrow one catches your ideal home the hour it lists; the broad one stops you from missing a good option just outside your filters.

## Managing them

All saved searches live under **Dashboard → Saved → Alerts**. Each shows how many homes match it right now across all listings. From there you can view matches, switch between Off, Instant, Daily and Weekly, or delete the search.

You can hold up to 10 saved searches, including flatmate alerts and similar-home alerts, and saving the same search twice does not use a second slot. Delete the ones you have stopped acting on — alert fatigue is the main reason people miss a match.

## Saved properties and similar-home alerts

Separately from searches, you can follow an individual property. Save it from the heart icon and it appears on the [Saved page](/saved). From there, the bell icon creates an alert for similar homes around that property's locality, BHK and price band.

Saving a property or a search requires sign-in, so saved homes and alerts sync across devices.

## Flatmate alerts

Flatmates has its own saved alerts for **Move in now** and **Team up**. They remember the active tab, locality, budget, move-in timing and sharing preferences, and appear in the same dashboard Alerts panel.
