In a tight locality, the good listings are taken within 48 hours. Saved searches exist so you are not refreshing the page.

## Saving a search

1. Apply your filters on [Listings](/listings).
2. Tap **Alert me** (the bell) beside the smart-search bar above the results. You will be asked to sign in first if you have not.
3. A pop-up opens with your current filters already picked. Adjust the deal, localities, BHK, budget, property type, furnishing and **How often**, then tap **Create alert**. Leave localities empty to watch every area.
4. Change the alert any time from your dashboard.

If a search finds nothing, or fewer than three homes, the page also offers **Create alert** so you hear about new matches.

## Alert frequency

| Frequency | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| Instant | Hot localities, moving in under two weeks. You hear as soon as a match is listed |
| Daily | Normal search, 1-2 months of runway. One summary a day (the default) |
| Weekly | Browsing, no fixed timeline. One summary a week |
| Paused | Keep the search saved without notifications |

Alerts reach you in the app, and only when new matches have appeared. When Draazy asks to turn on browser notifications, tap **Turn on** so alerts also reach you while the site is closed. You can switch match alerts off or set quiet hours in [Notifications and appearance](/help/a/notifications-language).

> [!TIP]
> Run two saved searches: one narrow and instant, one broader and daily. The narrow one catches your ideal home the hour it lists; the broad one stops you from missing a good option just outside your filters.

## Managing them

All your alerts are in **Dashboard → Alerts**, under **Property & Flatmate Alerts**. Each shows how many homes match it right now and how many are new. Use the frequency dropdown on a row to switch between Paused, Instant, Daily and Weekly, **Edit** to change what a property alert watches, **View matches** to see the results, or the bin to delete it. **New alert** starts one from scratch.

You can hold up to 10 saved searches, including flatmate alerts and similar-home alerts, and saving the same search twice does not use a second slot. Delete the ones you have stopped acting on, because alert fatigue is the main reason people miss a match.

## The Saved page

Tap the heart on a property, or **Save** on a flatmate post, and it goes to your [Saved page](/saved). Homes and flatmate saves share one list, newest first. Use the tabs **All**, **Buy**, **Rent** and **Flatmates** to narrow it, and the row under them to jump to your alerts. A home that has been sold, rented or taken down stays on your list marked **No longer available**.

On a phone, swipe a card left to remove it. You get a few seconds to **Undo**.

On a saved home, the bell icon creates an alert for similar homes: the same locality and BHK, with a price band about 15% either side. It uses the **Daily** default; change it in **Dashboard → Alerts**.

Saving needs sign-in, so your list follows you across devices.

## Flatmate alerts

Flatmates has its own alerts for **Rooms**, **Flatmates** and **Teams**. When the board has nothing for your filters, or you have narrowed it with two or more filters, the page offers **Create alert**. The alert remembers the tab, locality, budget, move-in timing and sharing preferences, and appears in the same Alerts list. You can pause, change the frequency or delete a flatmate alert there, but to change what it watches, delete it and create a new one from the board.
