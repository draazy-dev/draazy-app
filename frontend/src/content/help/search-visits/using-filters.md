The [Listings page](/listings) has smart search, live result counts and a mobile filter sheet. Applying filters in the wrong order is still why searches return either 400 results or zero.

## Start with smart search

Type the way you would ask a person: "2 BHK in Baner under 35k pet friendly" or "plots near Hinjawadi above 1000 sqft". Draazy turns the parts it understands into filters and keeps any remaining words as text search.

Smart search refines the filters already on screen. If it switches from rent to buy, the page resets to the right filter set for that journey.

## Apply filters in this order

### 1. Locality or place, not city

Pune is too large to search as one unit. Either search for and pick a few **Localities**, or use **Near a Place** — search for your workplace, school, landmark or society and choose a distance or commute-time radius. See [Map view and commute time](/help/a/map-view-commute).

### 2. Budget as a range, not a ceiling

Set both a minimum and a maximum. A ₹0–₹30,000 search buries the ₹28,000 flats you want under the ₹9,000 ones you do not.

> [!TIP]
> Set the maximum about 10% above your real ceiling. Rent is negotiable more often than people assume, and excluding a property at ₹33,000 that would go for ₹30,000 costs you options.

### 3. Configuration and carpet area

BHK alone is unreliable — a 2 BHK in Baner and a 2 BHK in Kothrud can differ by 300 sq ft. If space matters, filter on **area** as well.

### 4. The deal-breakers only

Furnishing, parking, pet-friendly, lift, power backup, security, food preference. Add these one at a time and watch the result count. If it collapses, you have found the constraint that is actually costing you.

## Filters worth knowing about

- **Available From** — narrows rentals by when the owner says they are available
- **Near a Place** — uses a real place and a radius, instead of trusting locality boundaries
- **Verification** — narrows to specific current [verification badges](/help/a/how-we-verify): **KYC done**, **Verified property**, or **RERA shared** (the owner shared a number you can check on MahaRERA)
- **Security Deposit** — filters rentals by stated deposit while keeping homes where the owner has not stated one

The result count above the listings and the phone filter sheet update for the current search. If it falls to zero, remove the last constraint you added. The empty page also offers one-tap ways to widen, such as **Widen budget** or **Any BHK**.

Switch between **Grid view**, **List view** and **Map view** above the results.

On a phone, open filters from the bottom filter button. **Alert me** asks you to sign in first and then opens a pop-up to confirm the alert; see [Saved searches and alerts](/help/a/saved-searches-alerts). Contacting an owner opens the sign-in step and then returns you to the same listing.

## Sorting

The default **Relevance** sort scores each listing on featured placement, verification badges, an owner-shared MahaRERA number, freshness and listing completeness. Featured placement is the highest-weighted term.

**Price: Low to High**, **Price: High to Low** and **Price per sq ft (low to high)** switch the relevance score off. **Newest First** and **Verified first** are their own ranked orders. Switch to **Newest First** while actively hunting; in a hot locality, being early matters more than being precise.
