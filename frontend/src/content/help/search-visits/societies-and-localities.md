Most people pick a locality first and a building second. Draazy has a page for each, so you can do that research without leaving the site.

## Locality pages

[Locality guides](/locality) covers 12 of Pune's most popular areas, from Hinjawadi and Baner in the west to Kharadi and Viman Nagar in the east: who each suits, how you get around and what to check before you rent or buy.

Each locality page also shows the homes live on Draazy there. Average rent and rate per sq. ft. appear once an area has at least three live listings; until then, the page says so rather than guessing.

From any locality page you can jump straight into [search](/listings) restricted to that area, or set an alert so new listings there reach you. See [Saved searches and alerts](/help/a/saved-searches-alerts).

## Society pages

[Explore societies](/societies) lists Pune housing societies. Search, filter by locality, and follow the ones you are interested in.

Each society has a page with these tabs:

| Tab | What is on it |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Specs, amenities, age, and a summary rating |
| Homes | Flats currently on sale or on rent in that building, on Draazy |
| Reviews | Ratings and reviews, shown once a society has three |
| Location | Map, connectivity and what is nearby |

> [!TIP]
> Follow a society even when nothing is listed in it. Following is how we know demand exists for a building, and it is what puts a new listing there in front of you first.

## Contributing

Anyone signed in can follow a society and leave a review.

## If your society is missing

Search for it on Google Maps from [Explore societies](/societies) or while listing a home. Societies are always picked from Google Maps, so each one matches a real place. Once added, you can follow it, review it and list a flat in it.
