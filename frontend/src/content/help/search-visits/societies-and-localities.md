Most people pick a locality first and a building second. Draazy has a page for each, so you can do that research without leaving the site.

## Locality pages

[Locality guides](/locality) covers Pune's most popular areas, from Hinjawadi and Baner in the west to Kharadi and Viman Nagar in the east: who each suits, how you get around and what to check before you rent or buy.

Each locality page also shows the homes live on Draazy there, plus **Resident reviews** you can read and add to when signed in. Average rent and rate per sq. ft. appear once an area has at least three live listings; until then, the page says so rather than guessing.

From any locality page you can jump straight into [search](/listings) restricted to that area, or tap **Get alerts for {locality}** so new listings there reach you. See [Saved searches and alerts](/help/a/saved-searches-alerts).

## Society pages

[Explore societies](/societies) lists Pune housing societies. Search by society, builder or locality, sort by Recommended, Top rated, Most homes listed or A–Z, and follow the ones you are interested in.

A society page shows only the tabs that have something to show:

| Tab | What is on it |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Specs, amenities, age, how living there works (water, power backup, parking, lifts, maintenance, pets, food policy) and a summary rating |
| Homes | Flats currently on sale or on rent in that building, on Draazy. Shown once a home is listed there |
| Reviews & Q&A | Ratings by category and reviews. Shown once a society has three |
| Location | Map, **Commute to work**, **Get directions** and what is nearby |

Many societies are added by people when they list or search for one, so some show only a name and a location ("Limited details"). Those pages have no Location tab until more is known.

> [!NOTE]
> Draazy does not verify societies. Details such as unit counts, maintenance and occupancy are as shared or as added, so check them on a visit.

> [!TIP]
> Follow a society even when nothing is listed in it. Following alerts you when a new home is listed there or prices move.

## Contributing

Anyone signed in can follow a society and leave a review.

## If your society is missing

Use the "Can’t find your society?" search on [Explore societies](/societies), or the society step while listing a home. Societies are always picked from Google Maps, so each one matches a real place. Once added, you can follow it, review it and list a flat in it.
