Locality names are a poor proxy for convenience. Wakad to Hinjawadi is four kilometres and can be fifty minutes. Search around the place you actually need to be near.

## Using map view

Open [Listings](/listings?view=map) or switch to the map toggle above the results. Map view is area-first: choose one to five localities, then the map shows pins from the current filtered results in those areas. Pan and zoom to inspect the pins; change the locality or other filters to change the result set. Tap any pin to see the listing. The map draws at most 100 pins, and says so when there are more matches than that — zoom in or narrow your filters.

## Near a place

In the filter panel, open **Near a place** and search for a real place — your office, a school, a station, a landmark or a society. Pick it from the suggestions, then set how far from it you are willing to be. You can express that distance two ways:

- **Distance** — in kilometres, up to 25
- **Travel time** — in minutes, up to 25

Both draw a radius around the point you chose. Travel time is converted to a straight-line radius at an average Pune city speed (about 0.4 km a minute) rather than a true drive-time shape, so treat it as a sensible radius, not a routed estimate.

> [!NOTE]
> Near a place deliberately ignores locality boundaries. A landmark sitting on the edge of a locality with no stock will still show you everything nearby on the other side of the line.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Whichever radius you pick, judge it against your **actual commute hour**. Peak-hour travel on the Hinjawadi, Kharadi and Baner corridors can be double the off-peak figure, and off-peak numbers are how people end up in the wrong flat.

## Commute times on a property

The **Location** tab on a property, and the same tab on a society page, show drive times to the main IT hubs when Draazy has the home's pin. They use live traffic when available and an approximate by-road estimate otherwise. A two-wheeler will usually beat it and public transport will not. The tab also has a **Directions** button and a link to that locality's page.

## Locality guides

Open [Locality guides](/locality) from the home page or the footer. Each guide covers who the area suits, typical rent ranges, how you get around, daily life and what to check before you rent or buy. Rent ranges in a guide come from published third-party data, not Draazy listings. See [Society and locality pages](/help/a/societies-and-localities).
