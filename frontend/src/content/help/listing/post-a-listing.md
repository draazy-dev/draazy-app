Posting is free and takes about ten minutes if you have your photos ready.

A free account carries **one live listing at a time**. Posting more is never blocked: an extra rent or sale listing is approved but stays paused until you buy it a Listing Pass (₹999, 60 days) or pause another listing — see [Plans explained](/help/a/plans-explained). Editing a listing you already have never costs a slot. Taking it down or pausing it frees the slot; marking it rented or sold does too.

## Before you start

Have these to hand:

- Photos of every room, plus the building exterior
- Carpet area (not built-up — tenants filter on carpet)
- Flat number, floor number and total floors
- Society or building name, and the pincode
- Availability date
- Rent, deposit and maintenance figures

## The steps

The wizard is four steps.

**Step 1 — Property details.** Whether it is to rent or to sell, and the property type: flat, independent house, villa, commercial, open plot or farmland. Commercial asks which kind; plots and farmland ask their own area questions. Residential asks for BHK and bathrooms. **Carpet area is required for every listing** — it is the one number every search depends on.

Draazy is owner-only: owners and family of owners can list; agents and brokers cannot.

If it is a rental of a residential property, you also choose here whether you are letting the **whole place** or looking for a **flatmate**. The flatmate route is a different flow — see [Finding flatmates](/help/a/finding-flatmates).

**Step 2 — Location.** For flats, houses and villas, start with **Find your society / project** — pick it from the Google Maps suggestions, or choose "Not on Google Maps" to continue with the address and map pin only. The locality follows the society you pick; localities come only from Google suggestions. If Google Maps is down, pick a society already on Draazy or a locality and we pin it for you; fine-tune the pin later from Edit. Then add the flat or unit number and a 6-digit pincode, and **place the property on the map** — a listing is never left on a default pin.

**Step 3 — Pricing.** For a rental: monthly rent, deposit and the available-from date. For a sale: price, possession and ownership type. Pet policy can be left unstated if you have not decided.

**Step 4 — Photos and description.** Add at least three photos, and label two of them with key categories (for a home: living room, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom). The first photo is the cover. The photo limit is set by the platform, defaults to **10**, and can be between **3 and 20**. One picker accepts JPEG, PNG and HEIC; large photos are compressed for you, so upload straight off the phone. Add a short headline and a factual description. Phone numbers and other contact details are not allowed in the headline, description or address — enquiries come through Draazy.

Then review and publish. Your listing goes into review and the status card shows **Under review** (usually reviewed within 24 hours), **Needs info**, **Live** or **Not approved**. If our concierge desk posted the listing on your behalf, it shows **Confirm your listing** until you tap **Review & confirm**, fill in anything still missing (including at least three photos) and tap **Confirm & send for review**.

> [!NOTE]
> The wizard saves your progress as you go, so closing the tab does not lose the form.

## Getting the Verified property badge

Verification is what makes tenants trust the listing enough to call — it is not required to post. To earn it:

- Tap the listing's **Get Verified property badge** button once it is live or under review. It opens your **Document Vault** for that listing: upload one ownership paper, then tap **Request Verified badge**
- For a rental, use a light bill, Index II, society share certificate or property tax receipt. For a sale, use Index II or the society share certificate
- Ensure the address matches the document

ID verification is a separate badge and is not needed for this one.

You get a notification when we grant it, or a reason if we can't, so you can upload the right paper and ask again. Your listing stays as it is throughout.

See [How we verify owners and listings](/help/a/how-we-verify).

> [!TIP]
> A Verified property badge and a fuller gallery are both one-time efforts that make a listing easier to trust before someone enquires.

## Fields people leave blank, and shouldn't

| Field | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Carpet area | It is a primary filter — blank means invisible |
| Maintenance amount | "As applicable" reads as a hidden cost |
| Availability date | Tenants filter on move-in date |
| Floor and lift | Decides it for anyone with elderly parents |
| Parking spaces | The number of spots changes the value materially |

## After posting

- **Needs info** means the reviewer needs one specific correction. Edit or reply from the status card to send it back. We remind you on day 3 and day 7, and archive the listing if there is no reply by day 14.
- **Not approved** is final unless Support reverses it.
- **Edit** stays self-serve. A change to price, furnishing, possession, address, photos or description stays live while it is re-checked; changing locality, property type, BHK or rent/sale takes it off search until review. Editing the address, society or rent/sale also removes the Verified property badge, so you will need to request it again.
- **Pause** hides a live listing from search without deleting it. **Resume** brings it back without a new moderation decision, unless your edits already queued a re-check.
