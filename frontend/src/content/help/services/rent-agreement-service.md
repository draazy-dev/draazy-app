[Rent agreement](/services/rent-agreement) takes the owner and tenant through a Maharashtra Leave & Licence agreement for a **residential** flat or house, from the first form to the registered copy. Our team drafts it, you both approve it, and we register it on the government IGR portal with a doorstep biometric visit.

## Who can start

- The **owner** or the **tenant** can start. Whoever starts invites the other side (see [Invite the other side](#invite-the-other-side)).
- The first step, Property, is open to everyone, and you see the estimate as you go. The later steps ask for PAN numbers and papers, so you sign in there.
- It is for homes only. For a shop, office, godown, or a company or firm as a party, use [Property legal](/services/property-legal) and ask the legal desk for a quote. See [Home loans and legal](/help/a/home-loans-legal).
- You can have one unpaid agreement per flat at a time.
- NRI and foreign parties are supported. See [Registration](#registration).

## What you are asked

The form has five steps: **Property**, **Owner**, **Tenant**, **Terms** and **Review**. It asks only what the IGR registration needs.

- **Property:** type, flat number, society, locality, city, taluka, landmark, police station, pincode, area, floor and road details. Village, CTS or survey number and zone are taken by our team from the Index II.
- **Owner:** name, PAN, mobile, date of birth and whether you are the sole owner, a co-owner or a Power of Attorney holder. Co-owners are all licensors and all sign, up to four. The owner uploads the **Index II**, which is required.
- **Tenant:** up to six tenants, each with name, date of birth, PAN, mobile and occupation. The tenant police record is collected here, because registration shares it with the police: permanent and previous address, an address proof type, workplace address with a work ID (not needed for students, homemakers and retired people) and the family members who will stay.
- **Terms:** start date, term (1 to 60 months), rent, deposit, any non-refundable deposit, yearly increase, lock-in, how the deposit was paid, who pays maintenance, who pays stamp duty and registration, parking, furniture, special clauses, and English or Marathi. Pick a preferred place, date and time slot for the biometric visit.
- **Review:** read it through and tick the declaration.

You never upload Aadhaar. Keep your Aadhaar and its linked mobile handy: our staff collect them at the visit for the OTP and biometric. You do not give witness details either; our staff record the witnesses.

Your draft is saved on your device as you go, but PAN numbers are not, so you enter them again after a refresh.

## What it costs

You see the estimate before you pay, itemised as:

- **Stamp duty (0.25%):** 0.25% of the agreement value, which is the rent for the full term (with the agreed increases), plus any non-refundable deposit, plus 10% of the refundable deposit for each year of the term. It is rounded to the nearest rupee, with a minimum of ₹100, as the IGR Leave & Licence calculator does.
- **Registration fee:** ₹1,000 in urban areas and ₹500 in rural ones. Rural applies to gram panchayat localities, and it comes from the locality, not from your choice.
- **Document handling (DHC):** ₹300.
- **Draazy service fee:** shown in the breakdown. See [Plans](/plans) for current pricing.

Government charges are paid in the parties' names at exactly the amount you paid us for them. They are not part of our fee. You can also try the [rent agreement cost calculator](/tools/rent-agreement-cost-pune).

## Invite the other side

The other side fills in their own details and papers, so nobody types someone else's PAN.

- **Owner starts:** invite the tenant by mobile. Each tenant can be invited separately.
- **Tenant starts:** choose **I'm the tenant — invite the owner**. When the owner accepts, the owner becomes the requester. They review and can edit everything, including your half, before paying.
- Draazy sends the invite on WhatsApp. If it does not arrive, use **Send it yourself on WhatsApp** or **Copy invite link**. The invite stays open for 90 days, and the person must sign up with that mobile number first.
- **Withdraw this invite** cancels it. If someone declines, you can **Invite someone else** or **Cancel this request**.
- The invited person sees **Action needed: complete your rent agreement** on their dashboard and taps **Fill my details**.

Only the invited person can edit their own side. The other side sees it as **view only**, with PAN numbers shown as the last four digits.

## Who pays

Once the form is complete, either of you can pay. Whoever pays first settles it. The payer gets the invoice, and any refund goes back to the payer. The tracker shows **Paid by** the payer's name, and the other side sees that you are waiting on the owner or tenant, not on payment.

Your request reaches our team only after payment. You can change your own details until our team starts work. After that they are locked, and changes go through **Message support**, **Request changes** on the draft, or a correction request from our team.

An unpaid request with no changes for 30 days is closed. If a paid request sits idle, we delete the PAN numbers we hold and the tracker asks you to enter them again.

## Track your request

The tracker is on the Rent agreement page. It has five stages: **Submitted**, **Documents**, **Draft & approval**, **Registration** and **Ready**. The status label on each request reads:

| Label | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Unpaid · not submitted | Nothing has reached our team yet |
| Waiting for the other party | The other side has not filled in their details |
| Submitted | Paid and with our team |
| Documents under review | We are checking each paper |
| Draft ready for your review | Open it and approve, or request changes |
| Changes requested | A party asked for changes to the draft |
| Needs your attention | We need a correction from you |
| Approved — awaiting registration | Every party approved |
| In government registration | We are registering it |
| Registered & ready | Your registered copy is ready |
| Cancelled | The request was cancelled |

If we reprice the agreement after you paid, the tracker shows **Revised terms** with the reason. Pay the difference and accept, or accept a lower fee, and any overpayment stays on record until it is refunded. We prepare the draft once you accept. Timelines depend on how quickly both parties provide papers and approve.

## If we send it back for a correction

When something is wrong, the status reads **Needs your attention**. We can fix most form fields and PAN numbers ourselves and tell you. We ask you only for a fresh paper or an answer.

1. Open **Needs your attention** on your dashboard and tap the **Rent agreement** tile, titled **Correction needed**. It opens the Rent agreement page.
2. Read the banner **Our team needs a correction from you**. It lists the reason and the papers to upload again.
3. Upload each paper again, and add a note in **Your reply** if you want to.
4. Tap **Done — send back to our team**. It turns on only after every requested paper is uploaded.

If the correction is for the other side, you see that you are waiting for them. A rejected paper also appears on the tracker for a new upload.

## Review and approve the draft

We share a versioned draft only after every paper is verified.

1. Tap **View draft** on the tracker and read it.
2. Tap **Approve**, which turns on after you open the draft. Or tap **Request changes** and say what to fix.

Every party must approve: the owner, the tenant and each co-owner. If anyone requests changes, we send a new version, and everyone approves again.

Check these in the draft:

- Names spelled exactly as on ID
- Complete unit number and society name
- Deposit refund timeline
- Notice period binding **both** parties
- Maintenance stated as a number, not "as applicable"
- Escalation on renewal

See [Rent agreement basics](/help/a/rent-agreement-basics) for what a sound agreement contains.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> In Maharashtra, a leave and licence agreement must be registered, whatever its duration. Do not stop at a notarised copy.

## Registration

After approval, we e-register the agreement on the IGR portal. Our staff visit at your preferred place and time. Each party gives a thumbprint and photo, and gets an OTP on their Aadhaar-linked mobile. We upload the registered copy to the tracker with its registration details.

If a party is an NRI or foreign national, Aadhaar e-registration is not available. We book a Sub-Registrar Office appointment instead. That party attends with a passport, or signs through a registered Power of Attorney.

Registering on the IGR portal shares the tenant's details with the police automatically. There is nothing to file or confirm. The tracker shows **Police informed** once the agreement is registered.

For a foreign national tenant, the owner must also file Form C on indiafrro.gov.in within 24 hours of arrival or occupation. Draazy reminds you but does not file it.

## After registration

- Tap **Download registered copy** on the tracker, or open it from the Documents section of your dashboard.
- Tap **View details** to see the filed agreement, read-only. Invited parties can open it from their own link.
- Where it applies, the owner's flat is added as a managed home, and the agreement shows under **Rental** on the dashboard with **Registered · view**.
- Our team checks the owner's ID before the registered copy is uploaded. The owner then gets the **KYC done** badge, and the linked listing gets the **Verified property** badge. See [How we verify](/help/a/how-we-verify).

## Renew an agreement

Close to the end date, your dashboard shows **Ends** and a **Renew** button. Tenants see **Your lease ends on**, with a button to renew with the same owner. Owners can also list the flat again if the tenant is moving out.

Renewing starts a new agreement with the property, parties, rent, deposit, term and start date filled in. Check the rent and dates, then submit. It is a new registration, with stamp duty and registration fee again. If flatmates change, use **Request a new rent agreement** from the flat.

## 50% off from a referral

When a friend you invited gets going, you earn 50% off the Draazy service fee of your next rent agreement. It shows at checkout when you are the one paying. Stamp duty, registration fee and other government charges are not discounted. One reward works on one agreement, and rewards do not expire. Your dashboard Billing shows how many rewards you have left. If you cancel that request, the reward comes back. See [Referrals and rewards](/help/a/referrals-and-rewards).

## Cancel and refunds

- While a request is unpaid, tap **Cancel request** (tap again to confirm) on the tracker. A cancelled request can be deleted.
- Everything you paid is refundable until the stamp duty is paid on GRAS. After that, the duty and registration charges are spent, and only the service fee or any overpayment above the quoted government charges can come back.
- Ask support for the refund with **Message support**. The money returns through the payment you used, to whoever paid. You also get a credit note.
- If everything you paid is refunded, including when our team does it, the request is cancelled.

See [Invoices, refunds and receipts](/help/a/invoices-and-refunds).

## Upload limits

- Draft and party papers: up to 1 MB each.
- Registered copy: up to 2 MB.

Images are shrunk to fit automatically. A PDF must already be under the limit.
