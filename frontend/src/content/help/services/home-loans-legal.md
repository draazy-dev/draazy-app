Buying is where the money and the risk both scale. Two services matter here.

## Legal title verification

[Property legal](/services/property-legal) connects you with the legal desk before you commit. Pick the service, say whether you are the buyer, seller or both, add the property location and an optional note; an expert calls you back within 24 hours. It covers:

- **Title chain** — 30 years of ownership history, establishing clear title
- **Encumbrance certificate and certified copies** — existing mortgages, charges and past registered documents
- **Sale deed drafting and registration** — the deed, Sub-Registrar appointment and execution
- **Stamp duty and registration fees** — computation, GRAS payment and e-stamp handling
- **Mutation and society transfer support** — municipal records, 7/12 records and society share-certificate transfer after registration
- **Gift deed, power of attorney and will** — drafting and registration for family and estate transfers

The legal desk confirms its terms with you before you proceed. Government stamp duty and registration charges are separate.

The page also has a stamp duty estimator. Within PMC and PCMC limits stamp duty is 7% and the registration fee is 1% of the value, capped at ₹30,000, on the higher of the agreement value or the ready-reckoner rate. The figure is indicative; the legal desk confirms the exact amount before registration.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Do the legal check **before** paying any token amount, not after. Token amounts are routinely non-refundable, and the defects that matter — a disputed title chain, a missing occupancy certificate — are exactly the ones you cannot see on a site visit.

## Home loans

[Home loans](/home-loans) compares offers across lenders on the same inputs. Choose the loan type (home purchase, plot, construction, balance transfer, top-up or loan against property), then add the loan amount, employment type, net monthly income and city. The loan desk calls you back within 24 hours.

### What determines your rate

| Factor | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Credit score (CIBIL) | Above 750 gets the best published rates |
| Loan-to-value | Below 75% LTV improves the rate |
| Employment type | Salaried at a listed employer beats self-employed |
| Existing obligations | EMIs above 50% of income restrict eligibility |
| Property approval | Bank-approved projects process faster |

Use the [EMI calculator](/emi-calculator) to sanity-check affordability before applying. A useful rule: total EMIs should stay under 40% of net monthly income.

### Documents you will need

- PAN, Aadhaar, address proof
- Last 6 months' bank statements
- Last 3 months' salary slips and Form 16, or 3 years' ITR if self-employed
- Property documents — agreement, index II, approved plan

> [!TIP]
> Get a **pre-approval** before you shortlist. It tells you your real budget, and sellers treat pre-approved buyers more seriously in negotiation.
