Rental fraud in Pune follows a small number of scripts. If you learn them, you will recognise an attempt within the first two messages.

## The one rule

> [!WARNING]
> **Never transfer money before you have physically seen the property and met the owner.** No exceptions, no matter how convincing the reason. Every scam below depends on breaking this rule.

## The five common scripts

### 1. The "I'm out of town" owner

They cannot meet you, but they will courier the keys once you send a token amount. There is no property, or it belongs to someone else.

### 2. The urgent token

"Three other people are interested, pay Rs 5,000 today to block it." Genuine owners hold a property for a visit, not for a stranger's UPI transfer.

### 3. The fake Draazy fee

Someone asks you to pay a "Draazy verification charge", "listing unlock fee" or "refundable platform deposit". **We never collect rent, deposits or token amounts**, and we never ask for payment over WhatsApp or UPI. Draazy charges appear on an invoice; see [Invoices, refunds and receipts](/help/a/invoices-and-refunds).

### 4. The photo mismatch

Photos show a far better flat than the one you are shown on site, and the story becomes "that unit just went, but this one is available". Reverse-search suspicious photos before you travel.

### 5. The rushed agreement

You are pushed to sign a rent agreement without reading it, often with a blank deposit or notice-period field. Never sign an incomplete document.

## Practical checks before you pay anything

- Visit the property in daylight and see the actual unit you would occupy.
- Ask for a utility bill or property tax receipt in the owner's name.
- Confirm with the society office that the person is the registered owner.
- Pay by bank transfer or cheque to a **named account matching the owner**, never to a third party.
- Insist on a written agreement listing rent, deposit, notice period and maintenance responsibility.

## If something feels wrong

Stop, do not pay, and [report the listing](/help/a/report-a-listing). If you have already paid, file a complaint on the [National Cybercrime Portal](https://cybercrime.gov.in) and tell us - we will preserve the account records for the investigation.
