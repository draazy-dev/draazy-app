[Flatmates](/flatmates) covers three situations: you want a room in someone's flat, you have a room to fill, or you want to team up with others to rent a whole flat. This article is for people looking. To offer a room, see [Posting a room for flatmates](/help/a/posting-a-flatmate-room).

You only need to be signed in to post or to show interest. Verification is a badge, not a door. The exception is a request whose author ticked the option to let only ID verified people express interest; you are asked to verify first. On Flatmates, **ID verified** is the same badge the rest of Draazy calls **KYC done**.

## The board

The board has three tabs:

- **Rooms**: rooms with a spot open. Move in, split the bills.
- **Flatmates**: people hunting for a room. Got a spare one? Say hi.
- **Teams**: teams forming to rent a whole flat together. Find your crew.

Use the filters (budget, locality, near a place, move-in, room for, looking for, sharing, trust and safety, private or shared room, furnishing, attached washroom) or the smart search bar. Under **Trust & safety** you can pick **Verified only**, **Gated society**, **Women-only homes** and **Men-only homes**. Switch between List and Map. The map works the same way as on [Listings](/help/a/map-view-commute): pick up to three areas, or search for a place.

Sort by **Best match**, **Verified first**, **Newest**, **Budget: Low to High** or **Budget: High to Low**.

## Ask for a room

Open a room and tap **Send interest**. You can say if you are coming **Just me**, **With someone I know**, or ask the host to **Find me a room-sharer**. The button then reads **Interest sent**. If the host accepts, it becomes **Message owner** and opens the chat in [Messages](/messages). If not, it reads **Not accepted**. You can also follow it under Dashboard, then Requests. See [Contacting owners](/help/a/contacting-owners).

**Save** keeps a post on your account. Saved rooms, people and teams sit in the same **Saved** list as saved homes, under the **Flatmates** tab.

> [!TIP]
> Ask to visit at the time you would normally be home. A flat that is quiet at 3 pm on a Wednesday tells you very little about weeknights.

## Post that you need a room

If you do not have a room yet, tap **Post**, choose **Flatmates**, then **I need a room**. Hosts then find you.

You must give up to three localities and your **Monthly budget (your share)**. You can also add move-in date, who you are happy to share with (**Anyone**, **Women only**, **Men only**), and must-haves: furnishing, parking, **Gated society only** and **Attached bathroom**. **More about you** is optional: age, occupation, room preference, a headline and a short note.

Your request goes live straight away and stays up for 30 days. You can have one live request at a time; take it down to post a new one. When a host who has a room taps your post, you see "{name} has a room for you". Accept to start a chat.

Phone numbers, emails and links in your note are refused. People reach you through Draazy.

## Living style

Answer a few quick questions about how you live: sleep times, cleanliness, guests, smoking, food, pets and the like. Skip any or all of them. Your answers are saved once on your profile and used when you post and when you search.

Once you have answered at least three, cards show a **% match** with people who answered too, and **Best match** sorting puts them first. Nothing is hidden for a low match. Answers show on a post's detail page only to signed-in members who have answered at least three questions themselves, and only for the questions they also answered. Edit or clear them from **Find your flatmate vibe** on the board.

## Team up and groups

If you do not have a flat yet, tap **Post**, choose **Flatmates**, then **Team up to rent a flat**. Fill in a title, up to three localities, the flat size, whole-flat rent (it shows what each flatmate pays), deposit, how many people are sharing including you, furnishing, move-in date, parking and whether the owner must allow bachelors. Tap **Create group**. It appears on **Teams** so people can form the household first.

To join a group, tap **Request to join**. Every join is a request: the host can **Chat first**, then **Accept** or **Decline**. You get a message that the request is sent and that the host may message you before deciding.

- A full group drops off the board. Anyone with its link sees "This team is full right now", with **See open teams**.
- You can leave a group, or cancel a pending request. The host can remove members.
- You can be in only a limited number of groups at once, including pending requests. The default is two.
- Group chat lives in [Messages](/messages).

Open a team to use **Find flats for us** or **See matching rooms**. When a group applies for a whole-flat rental, the owner sees one application with the group name, the host and the number of members.

## Alerts

On an empty board, tap **Create alert** to hear about new posts that match your filters. See [Saved searches and alerts](/help/a/saved-searches-alerts).

## What to agree before moving in

Put this in writing between yourselves, even informally:

- Rent share and who holds the master agreement
- Deposit share and how it returns when someone leaves
- Notice period between flatmates; one month is standard
- Bills: how they split, and who pays them
- Guests and overnight visitors
- Cleaning and any shared help

## Safety

- Meet in the flat, in daylight, before committing.
- Verify that whoever is collecting deposit money is actually on the rent agreement.
- Never pay a deposit to an outgoing flatmate you have not met. Settle it with the owner or the lead tenant.

> [!WARNING]
> Discriminating against a flatmate applicant on the basis of religion, caste, region or marital status is not permitted in listing terms. Preferences about food habits and working hours are fine; identity-based exclusions are removed.

Brokers are not welcome on this board, and the anti-broker checks are why a post can be held for a human to read, for example when two different people claim the same address.
