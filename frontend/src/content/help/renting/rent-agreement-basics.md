## Why 11 months?

Under the Registration Act, a lease of **12 months or more must be registered**. In most states an 11-month agreement avoids that requirement, which is why it became the default across India. **In Maharashtra it avoids nothing**: every leave and licence agreement must be registered, whatever its term (see below). The 11-month habit survives here because a shorter term means less stamp duty and a chance to renegotiate at renewal.

## Registration in Maharashtra

Maharashtra is stricter than most states. Section 55 of the Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999 requires **every leave and licence agreement to be registered**, regardless of duration, and makes that the owner's responsibility. If it is not, the tenant's account of the terms prevails in a dispute, and the owner can be fined up to ₹5,000 or jailed for up to three months.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> An unregistered rent agreement is not admissible as primary evidence in a Maharashtra court. If a dispute over deposit or possession reaches a court, the unregistered document severely weakens your position. Register it.

Costs are calculated under Maharashtra Article 36A. As a rough guide:

- **Stamp duty** — 0.25% of the rent for the whole term (with any agreed increases), plus any non-refundable deposit, plus 10% of the refundable deposit for each year, rounded up to the next ₹100 (never less than ₹100)
- **Registration fee** — ₹1,000 in municipal / urban areas, ₹500 in rural (Gram Panchayat) areas
- **Document handling charge** — ₹300, levied by the Sub-Registrar on every e-registered agreement
- **Service fee** — the Draazy service fee, shown before you pay

## What the agreement must contain

- Full names, ages, addresses, PAN and Aadhaar for licensors and licensees
- Complete property address including unit number
- Term: start and end date
- Rent, due date and payment mode
- Security deposit and refund timeline
- Notice period for both parties
- Lock-in period, if any
- Maintenance responsibility, itemised
- Escalation on renewal
- Permitted use, and any restriction on subletting
- Two witnesses, with their names, mobiles, Aadhaar and addresses

## Common mistakes

- Signing with blank fields to be "filled in later"
- No refund timeline for the deposit
- Maintenance described as "as applicable" rather than a number
- Only the tenant bound by the notice period
- Wrong or incomplete unit number
