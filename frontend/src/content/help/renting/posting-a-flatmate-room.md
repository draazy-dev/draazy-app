This is the host side of [Finding flatmates](/help/a/finding-flatmates). If you are looking for a room, start there.

## Post a room

1. Tap **Post**, choose **Flatmates**, then **I have a room to fill**.
2. **Room details**: **Single (1 person)** or **Double sharing (up to 2)**, attached or shared washroom, and how many **People living there now** (0 to 10). Say who the room is open to, any food preference (**Any**, **Veg only**, **Non-veg ok**), parking and amenities such as Wi-Fi, AC, washing machine, lift and power backup.
3. **Location**: find your society or building, which places the pin on the map, then check the locality. Say whether you are the **Owner** or a **Tenant** of the home.
4. **Rent & terms**: rent per month, deposit (required, type 0 if there is none), the date the room is free, notice period (0 to 180 days), minimum stay (0 to 24 months), and how maintenance and electricity are billed. A shared room's rent is split equally between the people sharing it.
5. **Photos & post**: add at least one photo, a headline (up to 120 characters; the app can suggest one) and a note. Tap **Post & Find Flatmates**.

People who like your room send interest. You see each one in [Messages](/messages) and under Dashboard, then Requests. Accept the ones you want to talk to and chat there. Your number is not shown unless you share it.

If you own a whole flat and have listed it on Draazy, **Let this flat room by room** lets you carve out several rooms from that listing.

## Waiting for our team

A room waits for our team to read it before the public board shows it, unless it is linked to one of your own verified listings. A post flagged for a shared or duplicate address waits too. You can still see and edit your post while it waits, and you are notified when it goes live or needs more information. Editing key details can send it back for review.

Phone numbers, emails and links in the headline or note are refused. People reach you through Draazy.

## Host standing and the Verified property badge

Every post shows a host standing that we work out; you cannot claim it.

| Host | How it is earned |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | You linked one of your own Draazy listings, and our team has already verified it. The room shows **Verified property** |
| Tenant | You are a sitting tenant, you uploaded your **registered** rent agreement, the owner agreed, and a person checked it |
| No badge | Everything else, including an owner without a verified listing linked |

Publishing a post and granting a badge are separate reviews. A live post does not earn a badge, and a badge does not publish a post.

### If you are a sitting tenant

1. Pick **Tenant** under "You are the".
2. Tick that the owner knows and agrees to you sharing, and enter the **Owner's mobile**. If you rent through Draazy, you can also link your tenancy.
3. We send the owner a short WhatsApp message about Draazy and your post, with **Yes, I agree** and **I don't agree**. You are notified either way. If they agree, the room shows that the owner confirmed it. If they do not, the post comes down while our team checks.
4. After posting, open Dashboard, then Documents, and upload your registered agreement. Tap **Get verified** on the room to request a review. Uploading alone does not grant the badge: once the owner has agreed, a person checks it first.

> [!NOTE]
> Only post a room the owner has actually agreed to.

## Expiry and limits

- A post stays up for 30 days. You get a reminder 3 days before, and when it lapses it comes off the board. Renew it from your dashboard.
- You can hold up to three live room or group posts, and one per address.
- A room or group for **Women only** or **Men only** does not accept interest from people whose profile does not match.
- A full team or room drops off the board until a seat opens again. Anyone with the link still sees it, marked full.

> [!WARNING]
> Discriminating against an applicant on the basis of religion, caste, region or marital status is not permitted. Food and working-hours preferences are fine. Identity-based exclusions are removed.
