## What is normal in Pune

| Term | Typical range |
| --- | --- |
| Security deposit | 2–4 months' rent (higher in Koregaon Park, Kalyani Nagar, Baner) |
| Notice period | 1–2 months, either side |
| Annual escalation | 5–10% |
| Lock-in | 6–11 months |
| Maintenance | ₹1.5–₹4 per sq ft per month |

These are market norms, not Draazy rules. Owners set their own terms.

## What actually moves in negotiation

In rough order of how often owners concede:

1. **Deposit** — often the easiest, especially for a longer lock-in or an earlier move-in date
2. **Notice period** — reducing from two months to one is common
3. **Escalation** — capping at 5% is achievable on a two-year view
4. **Rent** — moves least, but 5–8% is realistic on a listing that has been up a while
5. **Furnishing** — owners will frequently add or remove items rather than change price

> [!TIP]
> Negotiate one thing hard rather than everything a little. Asking for lower rent *and* lower deposit *and* shorter notice reads as unserious; asking for a one-month lower deposit in exchange for a 12-month commitment usually lands.

## Maintenance: get this in writing

Ambiguity here causes more disputes than rent does. The agreement should state explicitly:

- Whether maintenance is included in rent or paid separately
- Who pays the society maintenance bill
- Who pays for repairs — the usual split is owner for structural and fixtures, tenant for consumables and damage
- Who pays property tax (always the owner) and utility bills (usually the tenant)

## Protecting your deposit

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Photograph every room, the appliances and any existing damage on move-in day, and share the photos with the owner in a written message. This single step resolves most exit disputes before they start.

Also worth doing:

- Get a written receipt for the deposit, with the amount in words
- Pay by bank transfer, UPI or cheque to the owner's named account. Cash of ₹2 lakh or more in one go is barred by section 269ST of the Income-tax Act, and the owner can be fined the same amount
- Keep the payment reference: a registered rent agreement records how the deposit was paid (mode, reference, bank and date), and the amounts must add up to the deposit
- Agree the refund timeline in the agreement — 15 to 30 days after handover is reasonable
- Take final meter readings at handover, with photos

## Rent above ₹50,000 a month

If you are an individual tenant paying more than ₹50,000 a month, section 194-IB requires you to deduct 2% TDS and deposit it with Form 26QC against the owner's PAN. Ask a tax adviser if unsure.
