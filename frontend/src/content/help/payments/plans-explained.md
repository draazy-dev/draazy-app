**You do not need to pay to list a property or to receive enquiries.** Draazy is broker-free and only three things cost money, none of which renews by itself. See current prices on the [Plans page](/plans).

## What is free

- One live listing at a time
- The **KYC done** badge, if you complete ID verification, and the **Verified property** badge, if you submit an ownership paper
- Receiving and replying to enquiries; owners are never charged to answer
- Featured placement for a limited time after your listing's ownership is verified (the [Plans page](/plans) shows how long)
- Free flatmate posts
- Site-visit scheduling, and tracking a home you rent out with [managed homes](/help/a/managed-homes)
- 15 owner contacts, if you are the one searching. Use them within 30 days of the first

## What costs money

| Item | Price | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rent Listing Pass | ₹999 | One extra live rent listing for 60 days, tied to that listing |
| Sale Listing Pass | ₹1,999 | One extra live sale listing for 90 days, tied to that listing |
| Rent Contact Pack | ₹199 | 15 contacts for rent listings, valid 30 days; packs stack |
| Buy Contact Pack | ₹399 | 15 contacts for sale listings, valid 60 days; packs stack |
| Rent agreement | ₹499 + government charges | A first agreement or a renewal |

## Why a small fee?

- **Brokers kept out:** broker listings are flagged and removed, so you deal with owners only.
- **Every listing checked:** our team reviews each listing before it goes live.
- **Seekers can be verified:** tenants can earn the KYC done badge, and you can choose to accept requests only from tenants who have it.
- **Your number stays private:** owners share their number only when they approve your request.
- **No auto-renewal:** pay once, nothing renews on its own.

It is still far cheaper than a broker, who typically charges about a month's rent on a rental and 1–2% on a purchase.

## If you list more than one property

Posting is never blocked. A second live rent or sale listing is approved but stays paused until you either buy a Listing Pass for it (the rent or sale pass, matching the listing) or pause your other listing. Going live again on the free slot is free whenever it fits.

## If you are searching, not listing

The free account includes 15 owner contacts, usable within 30 days of your first, on rent and sale listings alike. If a serious search in a competitive locality spends them, buy a **Rent Contact Pack** (for rent listings) or a **Buy Contact Pack** (for sale listings). The 15 free contacts, the friend welcome gift and packs bought before the rent/buy split work on both. See [Contacting owners](/help/a/contacting-owners) for how the quota is counted.

## Saving money by inviting friends

Invite a friend on the [Refer a friend](/refer) page. When your friend gets going, you get 50% off the Draazy service fee of your next rent agreement. Stamp duty and registration charges are not discounted. Your friend gets a welcome gift too. Some rewards are checked for a few days first. See [Referrals and rewards](/help/a/referrals-and-rewards).

## Billing

Passes and packs are prepaid for the term and never renew. Every payment is under **Dashboard → Plan & Billing**.

## Money-back protection

- **Listing Pass:** no enquiries in the first 7 live days? Full refund, once every 12 months.
- **Contact Pack:** unused? Refund within 7 days.
- **Contacts:** if an owner declines or does not reply in 7 days, the contact comes back to you automatically.

Ask for a refund from **Plan & Billing**; the windows shown there are the live ones. See [Invoices, refunds and receipts](/help/a/invoices-and-refunds).
