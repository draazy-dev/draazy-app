**Paying rent through Draazy is not live yet.** The [Pay rent](/pay-rent) page is marked "Coming soon" and says "Rent payments are almost here". It does not move money, and there is nothing to set up. The plan is UPI, card or netbanking, instant HRA receipts, a deposit split into EMIs and autopay. Until then, keep paying your landlord however you do today. Tenants also see a "Pay rent · Coming soon" button on the **Rental** tab of the dashboard.

## What you can do now

### Record the home you rent

Open **Dashboard → Finances**, the **Rent Wallet**, and choose "Add the home you rent". Only the address, monthly rent and lease start are needed; the deposit and lease end are optional. You enter it once, and nothing needs to be logged month by month. The Wallet then shows:

- **Rent paid** this financial year, and the total recorded
- **HRA Tax Saver**: add your annual basic salary and tax slab to see your HRA exemption under Section 10(13A) and the estimated tax you save. Pune is treated as non-metro (40% of basic)
- **Deposit tracker**: the deposit you paid, how long it has been held and the expected refund
- **Your rent could be an EMI**: the value of a home that rent could service, with shortcuts to the EMI calculator and homes to buy

These figures are **your own record**. Draazy has not seen this money move and does not verify it, and the Wallet says so on the card.

### If your landlord tracks rent on Draazy

When your landlord tracks your home's rent as a [managed home](/help/a/managed-homes), the **Rental** tab shows your monthly rent, its status and the lease dates. Tap **I paid** after you pay, and your landlord confirms it with **Mark received** or "Confirm received". Each confirmed month produces a receipt PDF you can download, and receipts for a financial year can be downloaded together.

## What it is not

The Rent Passport, a portable record you could hand a prospective landlord, shows as "Coming soon". It can only be built from rent Draazy has actually seen move, and a record anyone can type is not evidence.

## HRA and landlord PAN

To claim HRA on rent above ₹1,00,000 per financial year, Indian tax rules require your landlord's PAN. That goes to your employer directly. Draazy does not collect it, though a landlord who tracks your rent on Draazy can add their PAN to the receipts.

If the landlord will not share their PAN, you can still claim by filing Form 60 declaration details with your employer, but get the refusal in writing.

## Receipts for your employer

Ask your landlord for a signed receipt, or use the format your employer supplies. **Dashboard → Documents → HRA Rent Receipts** generates monthly receipt PDFs from the landlord, tenant and rent details you type in (names, PANs, address, from and to month, monthly rent), ready for your landlord to sign. The Rent Wallet's yearly total tells you what the receipts should add up to, which is the usual thing people get wrong.
