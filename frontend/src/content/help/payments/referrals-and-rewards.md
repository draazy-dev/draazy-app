Inviting someone to Draazy earns you more of the thing you are short of — owner contacts if you are searching, listing headroom and free paperwork if you are letting. There is no cash reward, and there never has been.

## Your code

Open [Refer a friend](/refer). Your code is permanent and looks like `PUNE-AB12`. The page also gives you a share link, which is your code attached to the sign-up page.

Share the link rather than the code where you can — a friend who follows it has the code applied for them, and one less step is one less dropped referral.

> [!NOTE]
> Copying your code or opening the share sheet does not count as an invite. The counter only moves when a real account redeems the code, because a number that counts your button presses is not information.

## What you earn

| Track | Trigger | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Searching | Each qualified referral | **+15 owner contacts** |
| Letting | Every 3 qualified referrals | **1 free rent agreement** (Draazy fee; government charges still apply) |
| Letting | Every 3 qualified referrals | **1 extra listing slot** |

You are on both tracks at once — they are three offers sitting on the same referrals, not a choice you make.

## Invited, and qualified

The page shows two numbers, and they are deliberately different:

- **Invited** — people who have redeemed your code.
- **Qualified** — the referred owner whose first listing earned the Verified property badge, or a referral our fraud desk approved by hand.

Rewards are paid on **qualified**, not on invited. That is why the reward lands later than the sign-up, and why a batch of accounts created in one evening earns nothing. Past ten qualifications in 30 days, further referrals wait for a manual check.

Rewards are recalculated from your referral list every time you look, not banked. So a referral later found to be fraudulent simply stops counting; nothing is clawed back from a balance, because there is no balance.

## Where the reward shows up

- **Owner contacts** — folded into your allowance on the [Plans page](/plans). See [Contacting owners](/help/a/contacting-owners) for how contacts are counted.
- **Listing slots** — you can simply post another property.
- **Free rent agreements** — counted on the [Refer a friend](/refer) page as earned minus used. When you pay for a rent agreement with one available, checkout waives the Draazy service fee automatically and shows "Free with your referral reward"; stamp duty, registration and other government charges are still payable. If you cancel that request, the reward is returned. If a referral is later found fraudulent after you have used its reward, the agreement already delivered stays yours.

## If a code does not work

A code is refused when it is unknown, when it is your own, or when that account has already redeemed one. Redemption happens at sign-up only — an existing account cannot be back-dated onto someone's referral, so send the link before your friend joins, not after.
