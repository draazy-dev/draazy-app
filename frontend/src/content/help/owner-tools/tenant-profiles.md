Tenants can build a profile of their own: name, occupation, monthly income, who will live there (family, bachelor or company lease), move-in date, a previous landlord reference and a short intro. It helps them apply, but you do not see most of it.

## What you see

| What | Verified by us? |
| --- | --- |
| Name | No, self-declared |
| Mobile number | **Yes**, OTP at sign-up. Masked until you accept a number request, then shown to you |
| **KYC done** badge | **Yes**, the tenant completed ID verification (Aadhaar, PAN or driving licence plus a live selfie), checked by our team. Optional for tenants |

Owners see the tenant's name, a masked mobile and the KYC done badge, and nothing else from the profile page. Occupation, income, household and previous landlord answers are **not** shown in your Requests inbox, and none of them is verified, so ask for them yourself.

> [!NOTE]
> The badge tells you the person is real and traceable. It does not tell you their job, income or household are as described, so verify those before signing.

You can choose to accept requests only from tenants with the badge, using "Accept verified contacts only" in your settings. It is off by default.

## Sensible verification before signing

- **Employment** — offer letter or the last three months' salary slips
- **Identity** — Aadhaar or passport, matched to the person in front of you
- **Previous landlord** — a two-minute call, which owners underuse and which is the most informative check available
- **Bank statement** — reasonable for a high-value property, intrusive for a modest one

Ask for these once you have shortlisted, not in the first message.

## What you may not filter on

> [!WARNING]
> Listing preferences that exclude tenants by **religion, caste, region, marital status or disability** are removed, and repeated attempts lead to account suspension.

You *can* state genuine, property-related conditions: vegetarian-only kitchens where the society mandates it, no pets, family-only occupancy, working professionals only, no smoking.

The distinction is whether the condition relates to how the property is used or to who the person is.

## Police verification

Tenant police verification is mandatory in Maharashtra. Submit Form for Tenant Verification at the local police station or online through the Maharashtra Police portal, within 15 days of the tenancy starting. It is the owner's obligation, not the tenant's. If you use the [rent agreement service](/help/a/rent-agreement-service), the tenant step collects the details the police need, and registering the agreement on the government IGR portal shares them with the police automatically, so there is nothing separate to file.
