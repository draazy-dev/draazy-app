A **managed home** is a private record of a home you own. It is not a listing: buyers and tenants cannot see it until you choose to publish it. It gives you one place for the facts, the documents, the tenant and the rent.

## Where managed homes come from

A managed home is created for you when:

- you complete a Draazy [rent agreement](/help/a/rent-agreement-service) (the signed agreement is attached to the home in its **Rent Agreement** documents, and you are notified)
- you post a flatmate room
- you close a rental on a listing that was published from a managed home. Draazy needs the tenant's name and the monthly rent to fill the home in

You can also save a home from the free Rent-o-meter valuation with "Save as my property". It stays private until you publish it.

Find your homes in **Dashboard → My Properties**. Each card shows **Private** or **Listed to buyers**, an **Agreement verified** tag where it applies, "Rented to {tenant} till {date}" when there is a tenant, and a **Passport** percentage. Open one with **View details**. Only you can open your managed homes.

## Private, listed and Agreement verified

- **Private** means only you can see the home.
- **Listed to buyers** means you have published it as a listing. Use **Publish as listing** to open the listing form with the home's details filled in, or **View listing** once it is live. A home that still needs photos or details shows **Add photos & publish** or **Complete & publish**.
- **Agreement verified** means ownership was confirmed through a Draazy rent agreement. A listing created from such a home carries the **Verified property** badge. See [How we verify owners and listings](/help/a/how-we-verify).

## The property passport

The passport percentage counts five things, equally weighted:

1. **Basics:** locality, BHK and area
2. **Furnishing**
3. **Valuation:** open the valuation card and use **Get accurate valuation** if you want a paid report
4. **At least one document**
5. **Rent and tenant** if the home is rented (rent, tenant name and tenant mobile), or **occupancy** if it is not

The **Key facts** card lists the purpose, monthly rent or asking price, deposit, type, configuration, area, furnishing, society, occupancy and when you added it. The **Rent-o-meter** card shows an estimated rent, an estimated sale price and the locality rate per sq ft. It is an estimate, not a valuation certificate.

## Document passport

Keep the home's papers in one private place that only you can see. Categories are Title & Ownership, Society, Approvals & Plans, Purchase & Payments, Tax & Utilities, Rent Agreement and Other. Files can be PDF, JPEG, PNG or WebP.

## Rent tracking

On a rented home, open the rent panel and fill in:

- the tenant's name
- the tenant's 10-digit mobile (it cannot be your own)
- the monthly rent
- the due day of the month, from 1 to 28
- optionally, your PAN, which appears on receipts

Tap **Start tracking**. You can then:

- **Mark received** for the current month or any of the last 6 months. This creates a PDF receipt for that month. Future months and months already marked are not accepted
- **Invite tenant on WhatsApp**, to bring your tenant onto Draazy
- **Save changes**, **Stop tracking** (past receipts stay), or **It's vacant right now**

When the tenant taps **I paid**, you see "{tenant} says {month} rent is paid" with **Confirm received** and **Not received**. The tenant is told when you confirm.

Draazy does not collect rent. Tracking records what you and your tenant say happened; it does not move money. See [Paying rent online](/help/a/paying-rent-online) for the tenant side.

## When the lease is ending

As the lease end gets close, and for up to 30 days after it, the home shows "Lease ends {date}" with:

- **Renew** with the Draazy rent agreement service (the fee is the same as a first agreement)
- **Tenant moving out? List it again**, which opens the listing form so you can find the next tenant, or "List it from {date}" while the lease is still running

About 60 days before the lease ends, Draazy also sends a reminder to you and, if they use Draazy, to your tenant.

## Removing a home

**Remove property** deletes the home's passport, documents and rent tracking from your account. A public listing created from it stays live and is removed separately, using **Take down** on the listing. See [Keeping a listing fresh](/help/a/listing-freshness).
