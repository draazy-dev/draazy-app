## Notification channels

Draazy can reach you through:

- **In-app**: the bell in the top bar and the Notifications page
- **Email**: if you added an email
- **WhatsApp**: important updates, such as a request that needs your answer
- **Browser notifications**: Draazy alerts you through your browser, so allow notifications when asked. Shown only when your browser supports them

Manage them under **Dashboard > Profile > Notification Preferences**. For what else lives on that tab, see [Managing your profile](/help/a/manage-profile).

### What you can change

| Setting | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| New property match alerts | Get notified when new listings match your saved searches |
| Calls about homes and moving | Lets our team call you before your lease ends |
| Email | Match alerts and enquiry updates by email |
| WhatsApp | Important updates on WhatsApp |
| Browser notifications | Browser alerts on this device |
| Quiet hours | Pauses non-urgent match and price alerts overnight. Set the **From** and **To** times |

> [!NOTE]
> OTP and security checks may still appear even if you turn other channels down.

### Turning on browser notifications

After you sign in, Draazy asks once whether to turn on browser notifications. Choose **Turn on**, then **Allow** in the browser's prompt. If you chose **Not now**, turn on **Browser notifications** in Notification Preferences at any time.

With them on, you get a short "Draazy" alert for each new notification while Draazy is open in another tab, and for new messages even when it is closed. Alerts never show names or message text.

If your browser blocked notifications, the switch is greyed out. Allow notifications for this site from the icon beside the address bar, then reload the page.

### WhatsApp updates for owners

If you are an owner and requests are waiting on you (a contact, document, offer or visit request on a live listing), we send a WhatsApp message with the number of requests that arrived since you were last on Draazy. It goes out at most about once a day, and only if the **WhatsApp** switch is on.

To stop WhatsApp messages from Draazy, reply **STOP** to any of them. Reply **START** to turn them back on. Replying STOP does not delete requests; they stay in your **Requests** tab. See [Your dashboard](/help/a/your-dashboard).

## Appearance

Draazy opens in light mode. For a dark background, turn off **Dashboard > Profile > Appearance > Light mode**.

If animations are uncomfortable, enable **Reduce motion** in the same card. The app also respects your operating system's reduced-motion preference where the browser exposes it. Both settings are saved on the device you are using, so they do not follow you to another browser or phone.
