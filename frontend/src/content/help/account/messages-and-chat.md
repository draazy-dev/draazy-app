Open **Messages** from your profile menu or your [dashboard](/help/a/your-dashboard). It holds your chats with owners, tenants and flatmates, your requests that have not become chats yet, and the Draazy support chat.

## Your inbox

- **Draazy support** is pinned at the top, marked **Official Draazy team**, with an unread count. See [Getting help from support](/help/a/getting-help-from-support).
- Up to three threads show an **Awaiting your reply** pin.
- Rows for requests show a **Request** pill or the state of the request.
- Use the search icon to find a chat.

If you have none yet, the inbox says: "Requests you send or receive show up here, and become chats once accepted."

Press and hold a row, swipe it, or right-click it for **Archive**, **Mute**, **Block** and **Report**. Archived chats are under **Archived**, with a **Back to inbox** button.

## Inside a chat

The top of the thread shows whether the other person is online, typing or when they were last seen, and a chip for the property or flatmate post the chat is about. The three-dot menu has **View listing**, **Archive**, **Mute**, **Block** and **Report this user**.

- **Send**: press Enter to send and Shift+Enter for a new line.
- **Photos**: use the paperclip to attach a JPEG or PNG up to 8 MB, with an optional caption.
- **Message actions**: Reply (quote), Copy or **Delete for me**.
- **Drafts and offline**: your draft is kept per chat. Messages sent while you are offline are queued and sent when you are back online.
- **Phone button**: it works only when the full number is visible. Otherwise it shows a lock, because the owner prefers chat.

If you type a phone number or email while the number is hidden, you are reminded that numbers stay hidden until the owner approves. Use **Request number** instead. See [Contacting owners](/help/a/contacting-owners).

## Request threads

Requests appear in your inbox before they are chats.

- As the owner you can approve inside the thread. Approving shares your number and opens the chat. For a flatmate request, **Chat first** lets you check the vibe, and accepting adds them to your group.
- As the seeker you see "Waiting for the owner to accept your request" until they accept. The chat opens once they do.
- To counter an offer, open the listing. See [Offers and closing](/help/a/offers-and-closing).

If the owner deletes the listing, the chat is closed.

## Quick replies

When your message box is empty and it is your first turn, tappable suggestions appear above it. They change with the stage of the deal (contact, visit scheduled, visited, offer accepted), whether you are the seeker or the owner, and whether it is a rent or a sale.

- Seekers: "Is it still available?", "When can I come for a visit?" and, for rent, "What are the deposit and maintenance?" or, for a sale, "Is the price negotiable?"
- Owners: "Yes, it's still available", "When would you like to visit?" and "Sorry, it's no longer available"
- Flatmate chats have their own set.

## Privacy, blocking and reporting

- **Show when I'm online** and **Read receipts** are under **Dashboard > Profile > Chat privacy**. If you turn them off, you stop seeing theirs too.
- **Block** stops the person messaging you. You can **Unblock** from the chat. You will not be able to message someone you blocked.
- **Report this user** sends the chat to our team. Pick a reason and add details if you want. See [Reporting a listing or user](/help/a/report-a-listing).
- Alerts for new messages never show names or message text. See [Notifications and appearance](/help/a/notifications-language).

## Draazy support chat

Use **Draazy support** for anything about your account, a listing, a payment or a request. Choose what it is about from the **About** list (your cases and properties, flatmate posts, service requests, payments, or **Something else**) so the team sees the right context. Replies come in the same thread. Closed threads say so, and you can ask about something else.
