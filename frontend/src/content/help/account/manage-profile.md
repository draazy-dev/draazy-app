Your profile lives under **Dashboard > Profile**. Most of it is private.

## What owners can see

When you enquire on a listing, the owner sees:

- Your name and a masked version of your mobile number
- A **Serious Buyer** badge, if you have verified your identity

Your mobile is used for the contact request. The owner sees the full number only after approving the request. They do **not** see your email, your tenant profile details, your saved properties, your search history, or which other owners you have contacted.

## Your tenant profile

Open it with **Update profile** on Dashboard > Profile. It gives your profile a score out of 100: identity verification counts most (30), then occupation (20), income and prior landlord (15 each), and a short introduction and who will live in the home (10 each). Verifying your identity is what earns the **Serious Buyer** badge owners see.

You can fill in:

- Occupation
- Monthly income, if you choose to add it
- Who will live in the home
- Move-in date
- Prior landlord reference
- A short introduction

## Name and city

You can edit your name and city. While your identity is verified your name is locked to the one on your ID - to change it, raise a support ticket.

## Changing your mobile number

Your number is your login, so there is no self-serve switch for it. Raise a support ticket; support will tell you what proof is needed.

## Deleting your account

**Dashboard > Profile > Privacy & Account > Request account erasure** files an erasure request for review: click **Request**, type `ERASE` to confirm and submit. You stay signed in and nothing changes until it is approved. Once approved, it cannot be undone. If approved, your profile is removed and your account is erased where the law allows. Records such as invoices, legal documents, tickets and audit logs may be retained where required - see [Your data and privacy](/help/a/your-data-privacy).
