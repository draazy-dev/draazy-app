Your profile lives in the **Profile** tab of your [dashboard](/help/a/your-dashboard). Most of it is private.

## What owners can see

When you send a contact request, the owner sees:

- Your name
- A tick next to it if you have **KYC done**

Your mobile number is used for the contact request, and the owner gets your full number only after approving the request. They do **not** see your email, your tenant profile details, your saved properties, your search history, or which other owners you have contacted.

## Your details

At the top of the tab, chips show **Mobile verified** and either **KYC done** or **ID not verified**. You can edit:

- **Full name**. While you have KYC done, your name is locked to the one on your ID (**Locked while verified**). To change it, message Draazy support.
- **Email** and **City**.
- **Gender** (Female or Male). You can change it twice yourself, and the tab shows how many changes you have left. After that, or once you have KYC done, contact support.

**Mobile** is read-only because it is your login. See "Changing your mobile number" below.

The **KYC done badge** card has a **Verify identity** button. Verification is optional. See [How we verify owners and listings](/help/a/how-we-verify).

## Your tenant profile

If you are not an owner, the **Tenant Trust Score** card links to your tenant profile (**Update profile** or **Complete profile**). It gives your profile a score out of 100: identity verification counts most (30), then occupation (20), income and prior landlord (15 each), and a short introduction and who will live in the home (10 each).

You can fill in:

- Occupation
- Monthly income, if you choose to add it
- Who will live in the home
- Move-in date
- Prior landlord reference
- A short introduction

Owners see only your name and your KYC done tick from your request, not these answers.

## Settings on the same tab

Further down the Profile tab are cards you can open:

- **Notification Preferences**. See [Notifications and appearance](/help/a/notifications-language).
- **Owner contact preferences** (owners only): **Accept verified contacts only** and **Hide my number from approved buyers**.
- **Chat privacy**. See [Messages and chat](/help/a/messages-and-chat).
- **Appearance**.
- **Privacy & Account**: **Download my data**, **Sign out** and **Request account erasure**.

## Changing your mobile number

Your number is your login, so there is no self-serve switch for it. Message Draazy support; they will tell you what proof is needed.

## Deleting your account

**Profile > Privacy & Account > Request account erasure** files an erasure request for review: click **Request**, type `ERASE` to confirm and submit. You stay signed in, the request shows as **Under review**, and nothing changes until it is approved. Once approved, it cannot be undone. If approved, your profile is removed and your account is erased where the law allows. Records such as invoices, legal documents, tickets and audit logs may be retained where required. See [Your data and privacy](/help/a/your-data-privacy).
