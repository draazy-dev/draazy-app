Most things on Draazy are self-serve. When they are not, **Support** is the way through - it gives you a ticket thread you can come back to, which email and phone calls do not.

## Raising a ticket

1. Open Support and pick a **category**: Payments & refunds, Rent payment / HRA, Property listing, Verification / KYC, Account & login, Visit / booking, Home services, Technical / bug, or Something else.
2. Write a subject and describe what happened.
3. Submit the ticket. Keep the ticket id shown in the thread if you contact us about the same issue elsewhere.

Include listing names, order references or dates in the message when they matter. If support needs a document or screenshot, they will tell you how to send it.

## What the statuses mean

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Open | Raised and visible in your support thread |
| In progress | Someone is working on it |
| Awaiting your reply | We have asked you something and are waiting |
| Resolved | We believe it is fixed |
| Closed | Finished |

> [!NOTE]
> Resolved and Closed are not final for the conversation. You can still reply in the thread instead of raising a duplicate.

## Things only support can do

Some outcomes are deliberately not self-served, because letting either side set them would make them worthless:

- Reviewing a visit an owner marked as a **no-show** - see [Scheduling a visit](/help/a/schedule-a-visit)
- Reversing a verification decision
- Anything touching a refund

For these, raise a ticket rather than looking for a button.

## Before you raise one

Check the rest of this help centre first. If the answer is already covered, following that article is faster than waiting for a ticket reply.

You can also email [support@draazy.com](mailto:support@draazy.com) or call or WhatsApp +91 8728-8728-95, 9 AM to 8 PM, Monday to Saturday - but a ticket keeps the whole conversation in one place.

If the problem is a listing rather than your account - a wrong price, a broker posing as an owner, a property that does not exist - use **Report this listing** on the listing itself instead. See [Reporting a listing](/help/a/report-a-listing).
