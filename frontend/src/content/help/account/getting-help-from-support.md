Most things on Draazy are self-serve. When they are not, chat with **Draazy support** in **Messages**. Every support conversation lives in that one thread, so you can come back to it in a way you can't with email or a phone call.

## Starting a chat

1. Open **Messages** and tap **Draazy support**, pinned at the top. You can also tap **Message support** or **Ask Draazy support** on a listing, a service request, a payment or your dashboard. That opens the same thread with the right subject already picked.
2. Use the **About** dropdown at the top of the thread to pick what you need help with: one of your properties, flatmate posts, service requests or payments. Pick **Something else** for anything that isn't on the list.
3. Type your message and send it. We reply in the same place, and you get a notification when we do.

Each item in the dropdown keeps its own history, so switching between them never mixes conversations. A listing under review also shows its review status and any question our team asked you at the top.

## When a conversation is finished

When we resolve your question, the history stays put. If the problem comes back, write in the same topic again and we'll pick it up with the earlier messages still visible. A finished service request is the exception: its chat closes with the request. Pick another topic, or **Something else**, to keep talking.

## Things only support can do

Some outcomes are deliberately not self-served, because letting either side set them would make them worthless:

- Reviewing a visit an owner marked as a **no-show**. See [Scheduling a visit](/help/a/schedule-a-visit)
- Reversing a verification decision
- Anything touching a refund

For these, chat with support rather than looking for a button.

## Before you ask

Check the rest of this help centre first. If the answer is already covered, following that article is faster than waiting for a reply.

You can also email [support@draazy.com](mailto:support@draazy.com) or call or WhatsApp +91 8728-8728-95, 9 AM to 8 PM, Monday to Saturday. The chat keeps the whole conversation in one place, though.

If the problem is a listing rather than your account (a wrong price, a broker posing as an owner, a property that does not exist), use **Report listing** on the listing itself instead. See [Reporting a listing](/help/a/report-a-listing).
