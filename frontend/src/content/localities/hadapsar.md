Hadapsar is a large east Pune employment hub within Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) limits, with homes ranging from older housing to big gated townships. Published listing data puts 2 BHK rent at about ₹18,000–₹52,000 a month, a wide spread that reflects that range. It suits people who work in east Pune and want a choice of budgets.

## Who it suits

Hadapsar is big and varied. Professionals working at its IT parks and industries can live close to work, and families can choose between established neighbourhoods and self-contained townships. The range of budgets is one of its strengths.

## Getting around

- **Roads:** Solapur Road is the main artery into the city and towards Solapur. Magarpatta and the Kharadi bypass link it to Kharadi and Nagar Road.
- **Metro:** Hadapsar has no metro yet. Line 4, from Kharadi through Hadapsar to Swargate and Khadakwasla, was approved in November 2025 but has not been built.
- **Railway:** Hadapsar has its own railway station, though most long-distance trains use Pune Junction.

## Daily life

Seasons Mall and Amanora Mall cover shopping and films, and schools and hospitals are spread across the area. Solapur Road traffic is heavy at peak hours, so the exact location of your home matters a lot.

## Before you rent or buy

1. **Pick the right side of Hadapsar** for your office. A few kilometres can mean a long drive.
2. **Check the society's water supply and power backup.**
3. **In a township, ask about township maintenance charges** on top of society charges.
4. **For a new project, check MahaRERA.** See [how to check MahaRERA registration](/blog/check-maharera-registration).

## Nearby alternatives

[Magarpatta](/locality/magarpatta) is a gated township within Hadapsar. [Kharadi](/locality/kharadi) is across the river. Mundhwa, Wanowrie and Amanora are close by.
