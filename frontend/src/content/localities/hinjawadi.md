Hinjawadi is the Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park area in west Pune. It is not yet part of PMC or PCMC: gram panchayats, MIDC and PMRDA share civic work, and a merger into PCMC is still pending. Published listing data puts 2 BHK rent at about ₹15,000–₹40,000 a month; it suits people who work in the IT park and want the shortest commute, with roads, drainage and water to check before you commit.

## Who it suits

If you work in Hinjawadi, living there can save you hours every week. Freshers, flatmates and young professionals often choose it for the short commute and comparatively lower rents. Many newer societies have sprung up around the later phases, in Maan and Marunji.

## Getting around

- **Walk or cycle:** many residents live within a short auto ride of their office.
- **Metro:** Pune Metro Line 3 starts here and runs to Shivajinagar, with stations across the IT park's phases. The first stations received safety clearance in July 2026, and 17 of the 23 stations are planned for the first phase. Passenger service had not started as of 9 October 2026, and officials have tied the opening to the end of an election code of conduct, which runs to the end of October.
- **Roads:** the Mumbai–Pune Expressway and the highway bypass are close, but the internal roads are known for heavy peak-hour jams.

## Daily life

Hinjawadi has fewer restaurants, malls and hospitals than Wakad or Baner, though this is improving. Residents have long raised concerns about potholes, drainage, waterlogging in the monsoon and irregular garbage collection, and the state has announced plans for infrastructure work.

## Before you rent or buy

1. **Ask about water supply,** including whether the society depends on tankers.
2. **Visit in the monsoon or ask neighbours** about waterlogging on the approach roads.
3. **Check the commute to your exact phase.** Phase 1 and Phase 3 can be far apart in traffic.
4. **For a new project, check MahaRERA.** See [how to check MahaRERA registration](/blog/check-maharera-registration).

## Nearby alternatives

[Wakad](/locality/wakad) offers a fuller neighbourhood with a short commute. [Balewadi](/locality/balewadi) and [Baner](/locality/baner) are closer to the city, and [Pimple Saudagar](/locality/pimple-saudagar) is a settled residential option.
