Baner is a busy, sought-after residential and office area in west Pune, within Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) limits. Published listing data puts 2 BHK rent at about ₹28,000–₹45,000 a month. It suits IT professionals and families who want city life within reach of Hinjawadi, though that commute depends heavily on peak-hour traffic.

## Who it suits

Baner is one of Pune's most sought-after addresses for working professionals. It sits between the Hinjawadi IT park and the older city, so it suits couples where one person works in Hinjawadi and the other elsewhere in Pune. Families like the choice of large gated societies, and the cafés and restaurants along Baner Road keep weekends busy.

## Getting around

- **Roads:** Baner Road connects to Aundh and the University area. The Baner–Pashan Link Road and the Mumbai–Bengaluru highway bypass run along its edge.
- **Metro:** Baner will be served by Pune Metro Line 3 (Hinjawadi to Shivajinagar). The line was not yet open as of early October 2026, and the Baner stations are not in the first phase, so do not count on it for your commute yet.
- **To Hinjawadi:** a short drive on paper, but peak-hour traffic can make it long. Test it at your real office time.

## Daily life

Baner has plenty of restaurants, cafés, gyms and supermarkets, and it is close to Aundh and Balewadi for more shopping and dining. Metro construction along Baner Road has slowed traffic in places, so expect some disruption until the line is complete.

## Before you rent or buy

1. **Visit at peak hours** and do the commute you will actually make.
2. **Ask the society** about water supply, power backup and visitor parking.
3. **Compare societies, not just the area.** Rent and maintenance vary widely within Baner.
4. **Check the agreement and registration.** Read our guide to [rent agreements in Pune](/blog/rent-agreement-pune).

## Nearby alternatives

[Balewadi](/locality/balewadi) is next door with a similar feel. [Aundh](/locality/aundh) is more established, and [Wakad](/locality/wakad) is closer to Hinjawadi. Pashan is a quieter option nearby.
