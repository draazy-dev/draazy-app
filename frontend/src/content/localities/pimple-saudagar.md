Pimple Saudagar is a mainly residential suburb of gated societies in the Pimpri Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC) area, within driving distance of Hinjawadi. Published listing data puts 2 BHK rent at about ₹23,000–₹36,000 a month. It suits families and working couples who want a quieter base than the busiest IT corridors.

## Who it suits

Pimple Saudagar is mainly residential, with a large number of housing societies, schools and everyday shops. It suits families and working couples who want a quieter base than the busiest IT corridors, with Wakad, Aundh and the Hinjawadi IT park within driving distance.

## Getting around

- **Roads:** it connects to Aundh across the river and to Wakad and Hinjawadi to the west. The BRT road towards Wakad runs nearby.
- **Metro:** there is no metro station in Pimple Saudagar. Wakad Chowk on Line 3 will be one of the nearer stations once the line opens.
- **To Hinjawadi:** a reasonable drive outside peak hours. Test it at your office time.

## Daily life

The area has plenty of restaurants, supermarkets, clinics and schools within the neighbourhood, so most errands are close by. Traffic at the main junctions can be heavy in the evenings.

## Before you rent or buy

1. **Test the commute** to your office at peak hours.
2. **Ask the society** about water supply, power backup and parking.
3. **Register the agreement and inform the police.** Pimple Saudagar falls under the Pimpri Chinchwad Police. See [tenant police verification](/blog/tenant-police-verification-pune).
4. **Watch out for token-money scams.** See the [warning signs](/blog/rental-scams-pune).

## Nearby alternatives

[Wakad](/locality/wakad) is closer to Hinjawadi, and [Aundh](/locality/aundh) is across the river on the PMC side. Pimple Nilakh and Rahatani are close by.
