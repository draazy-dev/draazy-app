# Draazy changelog

What we shipped, newest first.

## 1.9.0 — 10 October 2026

**Design**

- Light theme is now the default. Switch to dark any time in your account settings.
- The home page hero is now light on the light theme.

**Search**

- Plainer search bars for the Localities and Near a Place filters.

**Guides**

- Pune locality guides with a map: homes, societies and resident reviews for each area.
- Pune property guides on renting, buying, home loans and rent agreements on the new blog.

**Owners**

- Listing a property: pick your society first and the locality fills in from it.

**Support**

- One support address, support@draazy.com, across the whole site.

## 1.8.0 — 5 October 2026

**Search**

- One filter sheet for listings, with the option to sort by verified first or price per sq ft.
- Filters now show how many listings don't state a detail, so silence isn't mistaken for a no.
- On phones, the Back button closes filter sheets, pickers and photo viewers.

**Saved and messages**

- Save homes and get in-app alerts when a saved search has new matches.
- Message owners and flatmate groups in Messages.
- Optional browser notifications you can turn on in Settings.

**Property pages**

- Full-screen photo viewer, YouTube walkthrough videos and a clear page when a property is no longer available.
- Ask to see an owner's ownership papers; the owner chooses what to share and access is time-limited.

**Owners**

- Pause a listing and resume it later, or edit and resubmit it after changes.
- Add a walkthrough video, pick the furniture and preview your listing before you submit.
- Phone numbers and emails can't go in listing text; buyers reach you through Draazy.
- Featured placement now comes with an active Owner Plus or Owner Pro plan.

**Flatmates**

- Detail pages for flatmate groups, rooms and people looking for a room.
- Group chat for flatmate groups, with lifestyle preferences and room details.
- Posts carry a title and expire after a while; some are held for a quick review before going live.

**Rent agreement**

- Invite your tenant (or the owner) by link to add their own details and documents.
- Pay before you submit; our team starts drafting once your payment is confirmed.
- Accept revised terms in your request tracker if the fee changes.
- Police tenant-information and Form C reminders after registration.
- Full refund until stamp duty is paid.

**Services**

- A services hub with a page for rent agreements, legal, home loans, interiors, valuation and packers and movers.

**Trust and account**

- Identity checks now include a quick liveness step: smile and turn your head.
- Verify your mobile number in a sheet without leaving the page you are on.
- Your dashboard shows what needs your attention next.
- Referral rewards: +15 owner contacts for each friend whose listing is verified.
- Draazy is now English-only; the Marathi interface and translated help are retired.

## 1.7.0 — 23 September 2026

**Verification**

- Verify your identity with a photo of your government ID and a live selfie, reviewed by our team.

**Listing a property**

- Commercial listings ask the questions that fit the space, including fit-out status and rent-free period.
- Plots and farm land ask for plot area in sq yd, guntha or acre.
- Floor plans come only from the owner; if none is shared, the page says so.
- Photos are resized on your device before upload (up to 20 per listing, 10 MB each).

**Flatmates**

- Smart search on the Flatmates board.
- Tenant-verified badge once a host's registered rent agreement is checked and the owner confirms by OTP.
- Hosts can choose to accept Verified Seekers only.

**Payments and plans**

- Pay for plans and services online by UPI, card or netbanking.
- Each plan now shows how many live listings it includes.
- Pay rent is paused for now. You can still record rent paid elsewhere in My Tenancy and download HRA rent receipts.

**Account**

- Refer and earn: share your referral code or link from the Refer page.
## 1.6.0 — 31 July 2026

**Help centre**

- New documentation and help centre at `/help`, with search, categories and per-article feedback.
- FAQs and product changelog moved under the help centre.
- Staff runbooks are now published alongside consumer docs, gated to internal accounts.

## 1.5.0 — 14 July 2026

**Search**

- Near a place: search around any landmark by distance or by travel time.
- Map view on the listings board.

**Owners**

- Listing freshness prompts from your dashboard.

**Fixes**

- Fixed carpet-area filter excluding listings where only built-up area was provided.
- Fixed the saved-search digest sending at the wrong hour for users who changed timezone.

## 1.4.0 — 26 June 2026

**Payments**

- Pay rent by UPI with automatic HRA-compliant receipts.

**Trust**

- Duplicate-photo detection extended across delisted listings, not only live ones.
- Report flow now accepts screenshots.

## 1.3.0 — 3 June 2026

**Flatmates**

- Group applications: up to four people can apply to one property as a single application.
- Room listings now show the existing household profile.

**Accessibility**

- Reduce-motion preference now respected across all animations.
- Improved focus outlines and keyboard navigation on listing cards.

## 1.2.0 — 12 May 2026

- Marathi interface translation.
- Society-level pages with amenity, maintenance and water-supply data for 900+ Pune societies.
