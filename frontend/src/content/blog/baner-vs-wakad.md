Choose Baner if you want a busier, city-facing neighbourhood with cafés and the older city within reach. Choose Wakad if you want a quieter, family-oriented area of large gated societies right beside the Hinjawadi IT park.

Published listing data shows Baner rents a little higher than Wakad for the same flat size. Both depend on peak-hour traffic for the Hinjawadi commute.

## Key facts

| Fact | Baner | Wakad |
|---|---|---|

| 2 BHK rent | ₹28,000–₹45,000 a month | ₹25,000–₹40,000 a month |
| Known for | Offices, restaurants, gated societies, Baner Hill | Large gated societies beside the Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park, Phoenix Mall of the Millennium |
| Metro (Line 3) | Not open as of 9 October 2026; Baner stations not in the first phase | Not open as of 9 October 2026; Wakad Chowk is a station on the line |
| Best for | IT professionals and families who want city life near Hinjawadi | Families and couples who want a short commute and a full neighbourhood |

## Who should choose Baner?

Baner sits between the Hinjawadi IT park and the older city. It suits couples where one person works in Hinjawadi and the other elsewhere in Pune, and people who want restaurants, cafés, gyms and supermarkets close by. Baner Hill and the Baner–Pashan Biodiversity Park give it some green space.

**Watch out for:**

- Peak-hour traffic on the way to Hinjawadi, which is short on paper but can be long.
- Metro construction along Baner Road, which has slowed traffic in places.
- Wide differences in rent and maintenance between societies.

## Who should choose Wakad?

Wakad grew alongside the Hinjawadi IT park. People who work there choose it for a reasonable commute together with schools, hospitals, shops and a wide choice of societies. It suits families, and couples who want more space than they would get closer to the city. Quick access to the Mumbai–Bengaluru highway bypass and the Mumbai–Pune Expressway helps with trips to Mumbai or Lonavala.

**Watch out for:**

- Busy roads into the IT park at peak hours, especially around highway junctions.
- Heavy traffic inside the area in the evenings.
- Water supply. Some societies around Hinjawadi rely partly on tankers, so ask.

## How is the commute to the Hinjawadi IT park?

Both areas lie on the Hinjawadi side of Pune, and both give a short drive on paper that can become long at peak hours. Neither is a guarantee of a short commute. Do the drive at your real office time, in both directions, before you decide. Our guide on [Hinjawadi, Wakad or Baner](/blog/hinjawadi-wakad-baner-rent) compares all three near the IT park.

## What about the metro?

Pune Metro Line 3 (Hinjawadi to Shivajinagar) had not opened as of 9 October 2026. Wakad Chowk is a station on the line. The Baner and Baner Gaon stations are not in the first phase, so do not count on the metro for a Baner commute yet. The status can change, so check the latest news before you decide.

## Does the civic body matter?

Yes, in a few practical ways. Baner is under the PMC and Wakad under the PCMC, so property tax and civic services come from different bodies. Wakad also falls under the Pimpri Chinchwad Police, so register your agreement and inform that police authority.
