The safest set-up for a shared flat in Pune is for every flatmate to be named in the registered leave and licence agreement with the owner. If one person takes the flat and brings others in, get the owner's written consent first. Then add a short flatmate understanding that covers rent, bills, deposit and what happens when someone leaves.

> This is general information, not legal advice. For a dispute or a large deposit, speak to a lawyer.

## Whose name should be on the agreement?

Everyone who lives in the flat. Section 24 of the Maharashtra Rent Control Act says the Competent Authority will not entertain a claim from a person who is not a licensee under the licence agreement. For a flatmate who is not named, that means very little standing if the owner, or the main tenant, disputes their stay or their deposit.

There are two clean ways to do it:

- **All flatmates sign the registered agreement with the owner.** Each is a licensee. The deposit and rent are one joint amount, and the owner deals with all of you.
- **One tenant signs and the others are named as occupants.** This is simpler for the owner but leaves the others relying on the lead tenant.

The first is stronger for flatmates. Our [PG or flat share guide](/blog/pg-vs-flat-share-pune) makes the same point.

## Can a tenant bring in a flatmate without the owner's consent?

Not safely. Section 26 of the Act says a tenant may not sub-let or give any part of the premises on licence unless the contract allows it. The Act deals with tenancies, and a leave and licence agreement is a different arrangement, so read your own agreement. In either case, the agreement is what counts, and most agreements bar sub-letting without written consent.

If you are the main tenant and want to add someone:

1. Ask the owner in writing, with the new person's name and ID.
2. Get the consent in writing. A message from the owner is better than a verbal yes.
3. Ask for the agreement to be amended or re-registered with the new name.
4. Make sure tenant details are given to the police, as in [tenant police verification](/blog/tenant-police-verification-pune).

Do not take money from a flatmate for a room you have not been allowed to sub-let. If the owner objects, the flatmate loses their place and you owe them their money.

## What does the owner need to know?

Owners worry about three things: who is living in their flat, whether the rent will arrive, and whether the flat will be damaged. A clear flatmate arrangement answers all three. Tell the owner:

- How many people will live there, and their names
- Who pays the rent, and whether it is one transfer or several
- Who to call about problems

If you are an owner letting to flatmates, ask for each person's ID, collect the police details for each and put a clause in the agreement on joint responsibility. Our [owner's guide to tenant screening](/blog/tenant-screening-owners-pune) covers the checks.

## What goes into a flatmate understanding?

This is a short private document between flatmates. It is not a substitute for the agreement with the owner. A page is enough. Cover:

1. **Names and rooms.** Who has which room and the monthly share each pays.
2. **Rent and bills.** Due date, who pays the owner, and how the others pay that person. See [how to split rent and bills](/blog/split-rent-and-bills-flatmates).
3. **Deposit.** Each person's share, and how it is returned when they leave.
4. **Notice.** How much warning a flatmate gives before moving out, such as 30 days.
5. **Replacement.** Who finds the new flatmate and who approves them. The owner must also approve.
6. **House rules.** Guests, noise, cooking, cleaning and shared supplies.
7. **Damage.** Who pays for damage to common areas, and how it is divided.

All flatmates sign it, and everyone keeps a copy. A photo of the signed page is fine.

## What happens when one flatmate leaves?

The agreement with the owner continues. Decide in advance whether the leaving person's share of rent is their problem until a replacement joins, or the group's. Many groups agree that the one who leaves pays until the new person starts, up to a limit.

The leaving person's deposit share is returned when the replacement pays theirs, or when the owner returns the deposit at the end. Put that in writing. If a flatmate leaves unannounced, the others still owe the rent to the owner.

## Before you sign

Read the sub-letting and guests clauses in your agreement. Check that every adult's name is on it, that the police details are filed and that each person has a copy of the flatmate understanding. It takes an hour and prevents most flatshare disputes.
