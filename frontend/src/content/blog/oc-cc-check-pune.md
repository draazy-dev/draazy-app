The commencement certificate (CC) is the permission to start building. The occupancy certificate (OC) is the authority's confirmation that the building can be lived in. For a ready flat, the OC is the paper that matters most, and you should see it before you pay anything beyond a small token.

Ask the builder or seller for both, and then check them with the authority that issued them.

## Key facts

| Item | What it is | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Commencement certificate | Permission from the competent authority to start construction; a copy is filed with the MahaRERA registration | RERA Act 2016, section 4(2)(c) |
| Occupancy certificate | Issued by the competent authority permitting occupation of a building that has civic infrastructure such as water, sanitation and electricity | RERA Act 2016, section 2(zf) |
| Completion certificate | Certifies that the project was built according to the sanctioned plan, layout and specifications | RERA Act 2016, section 2(q) |
| Who must obtain it | The promoter (builder) | RERA Act 2016, section 11(4)(b) |
| Conveyance deed deadline | Within three months of the OC where no local law applies | RERA Act 2016, section 17(1) |

## What is a commencement certificate?

A builder cannot start construction on a sanctioned plan without the planning authority's permission. That permission is the commencement certificate. For a new project it should match the project you are buying into: same plot, same building and the same number of floors.

Under RERA, a promoter registering a project must file an authenticated copy of the approvals and the commencement certificate (section 4(2)(c)). Our guide to [checking MahaRERA registration](/blog/check-maharera-registration) shows where to find the project page.

## What is an occupancy certificate?

RERA defines the occupancy certificate as the certificate, by whatever name called, issued by the competent authority that permits occupation of a building. The building must have civic infrastructure such as water, sanitation and electricity.

It is a different paper from the commencement certificate. A project can have a valid commencement certificate for years and still not have an OC, because the OC comes only after the building is finished.

## What is the difference between OC and completion certificate?

RERA treats them as two certificates that may be issued as local rules require. The completion certificate says the project was built as per the sanctioned plan, layout plan and specifications. The occupancy certificate permits people to move in. Local law decides which are issued and when, and the same document can carry either name. Ask the authority which certificate applies to your building.

## Who issues them in Pune?

The authority depends on where the building stands:

- **Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC)** for areas such as Kothrud, Baner, Aundh, Viman Nagar, Kharadi and Hadapsar.
- **Pimpri Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC)** for areas such as Wakad and Pimple Saudagar.
- **Pune Metropolitan Region Development Authority (PMRDA)** for many areas outside the two corporations.

Check the locality guide for the civic body in your area, for example [Wakad](/locality/wakad) or [Baner](/locality/baner).

## How do I check an OC?

1. **Ask for a copy of the OC** and the approved plan. Read the survey or plot number, the building or wing name and the date.
2. **Match it with the flat.** The OC should cover the wing or building your flat is in. Ask whether it is a full OC or a partial one for some floors or wings.
3. **Verify it with the authority.** Contact the building permission department of the PMC, PCMC or PMRDA, with the file or permission number printed on the certificate, and confirm that it was issued.
4. **For a new project, check MahaRERA.** The promoter must obtain the OC and make it available to buyers (section 11(4)(b)).
5. **Ask your lawyer.** Our [property legal services](/services/property-legal) can review the papers.

## What are the risks of buying without an OC?

An OC protects you in several ways:

- **Legal standing.** A building without an OC has not been certified as fit to occupy, and the authority has not confirmed that it matches the sanctioned plan.
- **Conveyance and society.** The Act expects the conveyance deed within three months of the OC, where no local law applies, and the transfer of documents and common areas to the buyers' association after the OC (section 17).
- **Possession.** An allottee must take possession within two months of the OC (section 19(10)). A builder who hands over possession before the OC leaves you exposed.
- **Loans and resale.** Banks and buyers usually ask for the OC on a ready flat. Our [home loan documents checklist](/blog/home-loan-documents-pune) lists it, and so does the [resale flat checklist](/blog/resale-flat-documents-pune).

Ask the authority what action it can take against a building without an OC, and what that would mean for you. If the builder says the OC is "applied for" or "coming soon", treat the flat as not ready.

## What if I am buying under construction?

You will not have an OC yet. Check that the commencement certificate and sanctioned plans exist and match the project, and that the MahaRERA registration is valid. Then insist that possession is not due until the OC is issued.
