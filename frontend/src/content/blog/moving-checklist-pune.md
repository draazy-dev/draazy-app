Start four to six weeks before the move. Sign and register the agreement, give notice at your current flat, book packers, and transfer electricity and gas, then update your address everywhere once you have moved. The steps below are in the order that avoids most last-minute trouble.

## When should I start?

Four to six weeks before you move is comfortable. If your current agreement has a notice period, count back from the date you want to leave. Read our guide to [notice period and lock-in](/blog/notice-period-lock-in-rent-agreement) before you give notice, so that you know what you owe.

## What should I do 4 to 6 weeks before?

- **Give written notice** at your current flat, as your agreement says, and keep proof of delivery.
- **Find the new flat.** [Browse flats for rent in Pune](/listings?deal=rent) and contact owners directly with no brokerage.
- **Check the basics:** the society's rules, maintenance and who pays what. See [who pays maintenance](/blog/maintenance-charges-who-pays-pune).
- **Ask about the society NOC** for tenants early. See [society NOC for tenants](/blog/society-noc-for-tenants-pune).
- **Declutter.** Sell or give away what you will not use. Fewer items means a smaller quote.

## What about the agreement and the deposit?

- **Get the agreement drafted and registered** before you move in. In Maharashtra, the leave and licence agreement must be registered. Our [rent agreement guide](/blog/rent-agreement-pune) explains it, and the [Draazy rent agreement service](/services/rent-agreement) can do it online. The [rent agreement cost tool](/tools/rent-agreement-cost-pune) shows the stamp duty and fees.
- **Pay the new deposit** by bank transfer and take a receipt.
- **Plan for two deposits.** Your old deposit may come back after you hand over the old flat, so you may have to pay the new one first. Our [security deposit guide](/blog/security-deposit-pune) covers refund timelines.
- **Give your details to the police** through the owner, as in [tenant police verification](/blog/tenant-police-verification-pune).

## What should I do 2 to 3 weeks before?

- **Book packers and movers.** Get at least three quotes after a survey, and read [how to compare quotes](/blog/packers-movers-quotes-pune). The [Draazy packers and movers service](/services/packers-movers) is one place to start.
- **Take an inventory** of your belongings with photos of valuable and fragile items.
- **Photograph the new flat** before moving in, noting scratches, cracks and the condition of fittings, and share them with the owner.
- **Start the electricity name change** for the new flat if the connection is to be in your name.

## How do I transfer electricity (MSEDCL)?

MSEDCL offers a "change of name" request on its Web Self Service portal and mobile app. According to MSEDCL's document list, a tenant applying for a connection in their name needs a legal document supporting the letting and the NOC of the owner. The application also involves Form X, under which the old consumer either transfers the security deposit to the new name or asks for a refund. The new name appears on the next bill.

At the old flat, tell the owner so the connection can be transferred or closed, and photograph the final meter reading. Pay any dues up to that date.

## How do I move or close piped gas (MNGL)?

Piped gas in most of Pune is supplied by Maharashtra Natural Gas Ltd (MNGL). If you are moving to a flat with an MNGL connection, the new occupier can apply for a name transfer, and MNGL processes it only after outstanding dues are cleared.

To stop gas at the old flat, send MNGL Customer Care at Shivajinagar a written request with a cancelled cheque. MNGL visits to disconnect, and the refund is processed after deducting any pending amount. The helpline is 1800 266 2696, from 8 am to 8 pm. Charges can apply, so ask what they are.

## What about internet, DTH and other services?

Call your provider two to three weeks before the move. Ask whether they can shift the connection, how long it takes and whether there is a fee. If you are leaving the provider, ask what equipment to return and get written confirmation that the account is closed. Also tell your cook, maid, newspaper vendor, milk supplier and any tiffin service.

## What should I do in the last week?

- **Pack a first-night box:** chargers, documents, toiletries, bedding, basic kitchen items and medicines.
- **Keep valuables and documents with you**, not on the truck: jewellery, laptops, passports, the agreement and deposit receipts.
- **Confirm the date, time and the lift or society move-in rules** with the movers and the society. Some societies allow moving only at certain hours.
- **Clear all dues** at the old flat: rent, maintenance, electricity, gas, and any society charge.
- **Do a joint walk-through** at the old flat with the owner, take photos, and agree what will be deducted from the deposit, if anything.

## What should I do on moving day?

1. Be present at both ends, or send a trusted person.
2. Tick off the inventory as items are loaded and unloaded.
3. Photograph damage immediately and tell the mover before they leave.
4. Take the final meter readings and return the keys with a signed handover note.

## How do I change my address after the move?

Update your address as soon as you have a registered agreement or other proof of the new address. Start with Aadhaar and your bank, then your PAN details, driving licence, vehicle registration, insurance, employer HR records, credit cards, and your voter ID. Check each organisation's list of accepted address proofs on its own website, because they differ.
