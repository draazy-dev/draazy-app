A bank gives you the lower of two numbers: what your income can service, and what the RBI's loan-to-value (LTV) cap allows for the flat's price. Your credit score decides whether the bank lends at all, and on what terms.

Work out both numbers before you start flat hunting, so you know your real budget.

## Key facts

| Item | What applies | Source and as-of |
|---|---|---|
| Max loan up to ₹30 lakh property | 90% LTV | RBI Master Circular on Housing Finance, 1 April 2025 |
| Property ₹30 lakh to ₹75 lakh | 80% LTV | Same |
| Property above ₹75 lakh | 75% LTV | Same |
| Stamp duty and registration | Not counted in the value the bank lends against (except homes up to ₹10 lakh) | Same |
| Credit score range | 300 to 900 (TransUnion CIBIL) | cibil.com, October 2026 |
| Income ratio (FOIR) | Set by each lender, not by the RBI | Lender policy; ask your bank |

## What is FOIR and how does a bank use it?

FOIR is the fixed obligation to income ratio. It is the share of your monthly income that goes on fixed repayments, including the new home loan EMI.

The bank takes your net monthly income, applies its own FOIR limit and subtracts the EMIs you already pay on car, personal and credit card loans. What is left is the largest EMI it will approve. The RBI's housing-finance circular fixes the LTV, not this ratio, so the limit differs between lenders and often with income level. Ask your bank for its figure.

## How do RBI's LTV caps limit the loan?

The RBI caps the share of the property's value a bank can lend:

- Up to ₹30 lakh: up to 90%.
- Above ₹30 lakh and up to ₹75 lakh: up to 80%.
- Above ₹75 lakh: up to 75%.

Banks should not include stamp duty, registration and documentation charges in the cost they finance. For homes costing up to ₹10 lakh, they may add them. In practice you pay those charges from your own money on top of the down payment. See our [stamp duty guide](/blog/stamp-duty-flat-pune) or the [stamp duty calculator](/tools/stamp-duty-calculator-maharashtra) for the amount.

## A worked example

These numbers are for illustration. Your bank's ratio and interest rate will differ.

**Assumptions:** net monthly income ₹1,20,000. Existing car loan EMI ₹10,000. FOIR limit 50%. Interest 8.5% a year, 20-year term. At these terms the EMI is about ₹868 for every ₹1 lakh borrowed.

| Step | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Income available for EMIs | 50% of ₹1,20,000 | ₹60,000 |
| Less existing EMI | ₹60,000 − ₹10,000 | ₹50,000 |
| Loan this EMI can support | ₹50,000 ÷ ₹868 per lakh | about ₹57.6 lakh |
| LTV cap on a ₹75 lakh flat | 80% of ₹75 lakh | ₹60 lakh |
| **Loan the bank offers** | Lower of the two | **about ₹57.6 lakh** |
| Down payment needed | ₹75 lakh − ₹57.6 lakh | about ₹17.4 lakh, plus stamp duty and registration |

Income is the limit here, not LTV. Now add a co-applicant earning ₹40,000 a month. Combined income is ₹1,60,000, so 50% is ₹80,000. After the ₹10,000 existing EMI, ₹70,000 is available, which supports about ₹80.7 lakh of loan. The LTV cap of ₹60 lakh on the ₹75 lakh flat now becomes the limit. A co-applicant raises your income limit, but never the LTV cap.

## Does my CIBIL score matter?

Yes. A TransUnion CIBIL score runs from 300 to 900. Many lenders look for around 750 or above for their best offers, but cut-offs differ, so ask your bank. A low score can mean a higher rate, a smaller loan or a refusal.

Before you apply:

1. **Get your credit report** and check it for wrong entries.
2. **Clear overdue amounts** and avoid missed card payments.
3. **Do not apply to many lenders at once.** Each application triggers a credit enquiry.
4. **Close loans you no longer need,** which also lowers the obligations the bank counts.

## Should I add a co-applicant?

A spouse, parent or sibling with income can raise the loan you qualify for, as the example shows. Most lenders count the co-applicant's income and check their credit history too, so confirm this with yours. Ask the bank whether a co-applicant must also be a co-owner.

## What else do banks check?

- **Employment and income stability.** Salaried applicants show salary slips and bank statements. Self-employed applicants show tax returns and business proof. Our [home loan documents checklist](/blog/home-loan-documents-pune) lists them.
- **Age and tenure.** The loan term has to fit before your retirement or the lender's age limit.
- **The property.** The bank's lawyer checks the title and a valuer inspects the flat. For a resale flat, see [documents to check](/blog/resale-flat-documents-pune). For a new project, [check MahaRERA registration](/blog/check-maharera-registration).
