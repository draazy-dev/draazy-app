## Is tenant verification mandatory in Pune?

Yes. The Pune City Police issue an order under **Section 163 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS)**, which replaced Section 144 of the old CrPC. It requires owners, and estate agents who arrange rentals, to submit tenant details to the police. The order is renewed periodically, so it stays in force.

The same rule applies to paying guest (PG) accommodation and to flats in Pimpri Chinchwad, which has its own police commissionerate.

## What happens if you skip it?

Owners who do not submit tenant details can face action under **Section 223 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)** for disobeying a public servant's order. This can mean a fine, imprisonment or both.

This is not a rule on paper only. Pune Police began action against 113 landlords and PG operators after a Special Branch drive on 8 August 2026, and the drive was to be intensified. Mint later reported 175 landlords and PG operators facing action in two weeks.

## How to submit tenant details online

### Pune City Police

Go to the [Pune City Police website](https://punepolice.gov.in) and open the Citizen Portal. Use the **Tenant/Paying Guest Information Request** facility to send the details to the police station concerned. Fill it in, upload the documents and keep the acknowledgement it gives you.

### Pimpri Chinchwad Police

For flats under the Pimpri Chinchwad Police, which covers areas such as Wakad, Hinjawadi, Pimple Saudagar and Ravet, use the [Pimpri Chinchwad Police tenant information page](https://pcpc.gov.in/tenant-info). You can submit it online, at the local police station or by registered post. An OTP is sent to the owner's mobile number to confirm the submission. The owner's address and the rented flat's address cannot be the same, and false information is a punishable offence.

> [!NOTE]
> You do not need a police "NOC" to rent out your flat. You only need to inform the police about your tenant.

## Documents to keep ready

Details vary slightly between the two forms, but keep these handy:

- **Owner:** name, mobile number, address and ID proof.
- **Tenant:** a recent photo, Aadhaar or another photo ID, permanent address, and workplace or college details.
- **Rented flat:** the full address.
- **Rent agreement:** a copy of the registered leave and licence agreement.

If several adults will live in the flat, give details for each of them.

## Does online agreement registration cover this?

Partly. The registration department's i-SARITA system is linked to the police CCTNS network, so when a leave and licence agreement is registered online, the tenant's details are shared with the police. The department says owners need not submit them separately in that case. Pune Police still urge owners to submit tenant details through their online systems, so doing both takes a few minutes and removes any doubt.

## When to do it

Submit the details before the tenant moves in, or on the day they do. Do it again whenever a new tenant or flatmate moves in. Keep the acknowledgement with your copy of the agreement.
