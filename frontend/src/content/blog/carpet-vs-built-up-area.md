Carpet area is the only one of the three terms that the law defines. It is the usable floor area inside your flat's walls. Built-up and super built-up areas are larger numbers that add walls, balconies and a share of common areas, so the same flat looks bigger and the price per square foot looks lower.

Always compare flats on carpet area.

## Key facts

| Item | What it means | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Carpet area | Net usable floor area, including internal partition walls | RERA Act 2016, section 2(k) |
| Excluded from carpet area | External walls, service shafts, exclusive balcony or verandah, exclusive open terrace | RERA Act 2016, section 2(k) |
| Built-up and super built-up area | Not defined in the RERA Act; used by sellers, with no standard formula | RERA Act 2016 (no definition) |
| What a builder must disclose at registration | Number, type and carpet area of apartments, with balcony and terrace areas | RERA Act 2016, section 4(2)(h) |
| Unit conversion | 1 square metre = 10.764 square feet | Standard conversion |

## What is carpet area?

The RERA Act, 2016 defines carpet area as the net usable floor area of an apartment. It leaves out the external walls, service shafts, the exclusive balcony or verandah and the exclusive open terrace. It includes the area covered by the internal partition walls.

A simple test: carpet area is the floor you could cover with a carpet, wall to wall, inside the flat.

## What is built-up area?

Built-up area is not a term in the RERA Act. In common use it means the carpet area plus the thickness of the walls and, usually, the balcony. Because there is no legal formula, two builders can use it differently. Ask what is included.

## What is super built-up area?

Super built-up area, also called saleable area, adds a share of common areas to built-up area: lobbies, staircases, lifts and sometimes the clubhouse. The RERA Act does not define it either.

The difference between super built-up and carpet area is often called the loading. The Act has no rule for it, so treat any brochure figure as the seller's own number.

## What must a builder quote under MahaRERA?

Under section 4(2)(h) of the RERA Act, a promoter registering a project must state the number, type and carpet area of the apartments for sale, along with the area of any exclusive balcony or verandah and exclusive open terrace. The proforma agreement for sale is filed with the registration under section 4(2)(g). The agreement itself must follow the form prescribed by the state under section 13(2).

So the carpet area on the project's MahaRERA page is the figure to hold the builder to. If a brochure or the sales team quotes something larger, ask for the carpet area in writing and match it with the agreement. Section 12 makes a promoter liable to compensate a buyer who suffers loss because of an incorrect or false statement in an advertisement or prospectus.

MahaRERA project pages normally give areas in square metres. Multiply by 10.764 to get square feet. Our guide to [checking MahaRERA registration](/blog/check-maharera-registration) shows how to find the page.

## An example: the same flat, two prices

The numbers below are made up to show the effect. They are not Pune market rates.

| Item | Quoted on super built-up | Quoted on carpet |
|---|---|---|
| Flat price | ₹80,00,000 | ₹80,00,000 |
| Area used | 1,000 sq ft | 700 sq ft |
| **Price per sq ft** | **₹8,000** | **about ₹11,429** |

The flat and the price are the same. Only the area used changed. If you compare this flat with another that is advertised on carpet area at ₹10,500 a square foot, the first one looks cheaper but is not.

## How do I compare flats fairly?

1. **Convert every offer to carpet area.** Ask for it in the agreement, not only in the brochure.
2. **Check what is outside the carpet area.** A large balcony or terrace is useful, but it is counted separately.
3. **Divide the total price by carpet area.** Include parking and other charges in the total if you have to pay them.
4. **Measure on site.** Use a tape or a measuring app on a ready flat and compare it with the agreement.
5. **For a resale flat,** ask the seller for the carpet area in the sale papers. Older agreements may quote only built-up area.

## Does carpet area matter for rent too?

Yes. A 2 BHK with a smaller carpet area can feel cramped even when the listing says 1,100 sq ft. When you list or search on Draazy, look for the carpet area. ## What about stamp duty?

Stamp duty is charged on the higher of the agreement value and the government's ready reckoner value. The ready reckoner has its own area rules, so do not compare your agreement's area with it directly.
