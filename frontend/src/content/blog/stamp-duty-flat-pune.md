Stamp duty on a flat in PMC or PCMC limits is **7%** of its value (5% stamp duty, 1% local body tax and 1% metro cess), or **6%** when the women buyers' concession applies. You also pay a registration fee of 1%, capped at **₹30,000**. On an ₹80 lakh flat that is ₹5,90,000 at the standard rate. Most banks do not include these costs in the home loan.

## Key facts

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Stamp duty, PMC and PCMC | 7% (5% stamp duty + 1% LBT + 1% metro cess) |
| Women buyers | 1% concession, so 6% |
| Registration fee | 1% of the value, capped at ₹30,000, so flats worth ₹30 lakh or more pay ₹30,000 |
| Duty is charged on | The higher of the agreement value and the ready reckoner value |

## Stamp duty in Pune city and PCMC

For a flat within Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) or Pimpri Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC) limits, the total stamp duty is **7%** of the property's value. It is made up of three parts:

| Component | Rate |
|---|---|
| Stamp duty | 5% |
| Local body tax (LBT) | 1% |
| Metro cess | 1% |
| **Total** | **7%** |

Rates differ for property outside municipal limits, such as in gram panchayat areas, so check the IGR website for those.

## The concession for women buyers

Maharashtra gives women buyers a **1% concession** on stamp duty for residential property, which brings the rate in Pune to **6%**. It applies when the property is registered in a woman's name. If you are buying jointly with a man, the concession can work differently, so check the IGR calculator or ask the sub-registrar before you plan the budget.

Earlier, a woman who used the concession could not sell the flat to a man for 15 years. The state government removed that condition in 2023, so the concession no longer restricts resale.

## Registration fee

On top of stamp duty, you pay a **registration fee of 1%** of the property's value, capped at **₹30,000**. So any flat worth ₹30 lakh or more pays the full ₹30,000.

## Which value is the duty charged on?

Stamp duty is charged on the **higher** of two values:

- the **agreement value**, that is, the price in your sale agreement, and
- the **ready reckoner value**, the government's minimum rate for that area, published every year.

If you agree a price below the ready reckoner value, you still pay duty on the ready reckoner value. The Maharashtra government kept ready reckoner rates for 2026–27 unchanged from the previous year. You can look up the rate for any area on the IGR website.

## Worked example

Say you buy an ₹80 lakh flat in Wakad, which is within PCMC limits:

| Item | Standard rate | Women buyers |
|---|---|---|
| Stamp duty | ₹5,60,000 (7%) | ₹4,80,000 (6%) |
| Registration fee | ₹30,000 | ₹30,000 |
| **Total** | **₹5,90,000** | **₹5,10,000** |

Add legal fees and any service charges for document handling to this.

## How and when you pay

Stamp duty and the registration fee are paid when the sale deed or agreement for sale is registered at the sub-registrar's office. You can pay online through the government's GRAS portal and carry the challan with you. Your bank, builder or lawyer will usually guide you through the appointment.
