Rent in Kharadi if you work at EON IT Park or the World Trade Center and want a newer, larger society. Rent in Viman Nagar if you fly often or want restaurants and an established neighbourhood, usually at a higher rent. Neither area has a metro station yet. The nearest is Ramwadi, the eastern end of Metro Line 2.

## Kharadi: walk to the IT park

Kharadi has some of Pune's largest office campuses, including EON IT Park and the World Trade Center. Living here can mean a commute of a few minutes, which counts for a lot during peak hours on Nagar Road.

**Good for:** IT professionals working in Kharadi, flatmates and families who want a newer flat with society amenities.

**Watch out for:**

- Traffic on Nagar Road and the roads leading to it, especially at office hours.
- Construction dust and road work in some pockets as the area keeps growing.
- Distance from the older city, so trips to central Pune take time.

## Viman Nagar: near the airport and the action

Viman Nagar is a short drive from Pune Airport and has one of the city's busiest stretches of restaurants, cafés and shops, with Phoenix Marketcity nearby. Schools, hospitals and colleges are close at hand.

**Good for:** frequent flyers, people who work in Kalyani Nagar, Yerawada or the city, and anyone who values evenings out.

**Watch out for:**

- Higher rents for comparable flats.
- Aircraft noise in some pockets close to the runway. Visit at different times of day.
- Older buildings in parts of the area, so check the condition of the flat and the society.

[See the Viman Nagar locality guide](/locality/viman-nagar) or [browse rentals in Viman Nagar](/rent/viman-nagar).

## The metro: coming, not here yet

Neither Kharadi nor Viman Nagar has a metro station today. The nearest is **Ramwadi**, the eastern end of Line 2 (Vanaz to Ramwadi).

In June 2025, the Union Cabinet approved an extension of Line 2 from Ramwadi to Wagholi/Vitthalwadi, which is meant to serve Kharadi along the way. The approval set a four-year completion schedule. Groundwork began in September 2026, so do not choose a flat on the assumption that a station will open soon.

## Nagar Road traffic

Nagar Road is one of Pune's busiest roads, and work on flyovers and the metro extension has added to delays. Before you decide:

1. **Do your commute at your real office time,** in both directions.
2. **Check the route to the airport** if you travel often. A short distance on the map can take much longer at peak hours.
3. **Ask the society** about water supply, power backup and parking.

## Indicative rents

Rents change with the society, floor and furnishing, so compare live listings rather than relying on averages. Our [Pune rent report 2026](/blog/pune-rent-report-2026) has cited rent ranges by locality. As a rough guide, Viman Nagar tends to cost more than Kharadi for a similar flat, and both are among the pricier areas of east Pune. Nearby [Hadapsar and Magarpatta](/locality/magarpatta) and Wagholi can offer more space for the money.
