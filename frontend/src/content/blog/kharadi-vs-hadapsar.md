Choose Kharadi if you work at EON IT Park or the World Trade Center and want a newer gated society close to your office. Choose Hadapsar if you want a wider choice of budgets and housing types, from older neighbourhoods to large townships.

Both areas are in the Pune Municipal Corporation and neither has a metro station yet. Published ranges show Hadapsar can cost much less for a 1 or 2 BHK, but the range is wide.

## Who should choose Kharadi?

Kharadi has grown fast around its office parks, and many banks and technology firms have offices here. Living nearby saves a lot of time on Nagar Road. It suits professionals who work in the area and families who prefer newer societies with clubhouses and open spaces.

**Watch out for:**

- Nagar Road traffic, which is the main complaint. Road and metro work has added to delays.
- Higher starting rents than Hadapsar, based on the published ranges.

For a closer look at Kharadi against Viman Nagar, read [Kharadi vs Viman Nagar](/blog/kharadi-vs-viman-nagar).

## Who should choose Hadapsar?

Hadapsar is a large, varied hub. People who work at Magarpatta CyberCity, SP Infocity, the industrial estate or the Serum Institute can live close to work. Families can choose between established neighbourhoods and self-contained townships such as [Magarpatta](/locality/magarpatta). Seasons Mall and Amanora Mall cover shopping and films.

**Watch out for:**

- Heavy Solapur Road traffic at peak hours, so the exact location of your home matters.
- Township maintenance charges, which apply on top of society charges. Read [who pays maintenance](/blog/maintenance-charges-who-pays-pune) before you agree on the rent.
- A very wide spread of quality and price. Visit the exact building rather than judging by the area.

## How do the commutes compare?

Both areas depend on roads. Nagar Road connects Kharadi to Viman Nagar, the airport and the city, with a drive of about 15 minutes to the airport outside peak hours. The Kharadi bypass links it towards Mundhwa and Hadapsar. Hadapsar's main artery is Solapur Road into the city, and Magarpatta and the Kharadi bypass link it to Kharadi and Nagar Road. Hadapsar also has its own railway station, though most long-distance trains use Pune Junction.

Test the drive at your real office time before you sign anything.

## What about the metro?

Neither area has a metro station yet, as of 9 October 2026.

- **Kharadi:** the nearest open station is Ramwadi on Line 2. An extension from Ramwadi towards Wagholi, passing Kharadi, was approved in June 2025 and is at an early stage.
- **Hadapsar:** Line 4, from Kharadi through Hadapsar to Swargate and Khadakwasla, was approved in November 2025 and has not been built.

Do not choose either area for a metro that is not built. Treat it as a possible future benefit only.
