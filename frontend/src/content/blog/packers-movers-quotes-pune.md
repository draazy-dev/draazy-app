Get at least three written quotes, each based on a survey of your belongings, not a phone guess. Compare them line by line: inventory, packing material, vehicle, insurance, tax and the payment schedule. The cheapest number is often the one that leaves things out. Never pay the full amount before the goods arrive.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This is general guidance. Read the movers' terms before you sign, and keep all papers until your goods are unpacked and checked.

## How many quotes should you get?

Three is a good number. Two quotes tell you little, and more than four become hard to compare. Ask for each quote on the same list of items so you are comparing like with like. Draazy's [packers and movers service](/services/packers-movers) is one place to request a quote, and you should still compare it with others.

## Why does the survey matter?

A quote given over the phone for "a 2 BHK" is a guess. The final bill often rises when the truck arrives. A survey, in person or by video call, lets the mover count your furniture, appliances and boxes, check the floors, lifts and parking at both ends, and quote for the real job.

Walk the mover through everything, including the balcony, loft and storage. Point out heavy or awkward items such as a washing machine, a double bed or a wardrobe that has to be taken apart.

## What should the inventory list include?

Ask for an inventory list, and sign it. It should show:

- Every large item, with its condition
- The number of boxes, and which room each belongs to
- Fragile items and anything valuable
- Items to be dismantled and reassembled, such as beds, wardrobes and TVs on brackets

Take photos of valuable and fragile items before packing. You will need the list and the photos if something is damaged or goes missing.

## What should a written quote contain?

Compare these lines across all the quotes:

| Line | What to check |
|---|---|
| Services | Packing, loading, transport, unloading, unpacking, dismantling and assembly |
| Material | Cartons, bubble wrap, blankets, what is included and what is extra |
| Vehicle and crew | Size of truck, number of workers |
| Distance and floors | Charges for stairs, long carry or no lift access |
| Date and timing | Pickup date, delivery window |
| Insurance | Whether it is included, the amount covered and the policy |
| Tax | Whether tax is included in the total |
| Extras | Waiting time, parking, society charges, tolls |
| Payment | Amount upfront and when the balance is due |

Do not pick on the total alone. A quote that is lower because it excludes packing material or insurance is not cheaper.

## What is transit insurance, and do you need it?

Transit insurance covers loss or damage to your goods while they are on the move. A mover's own liability for damage is usually limited, and the terms differ, so read the liability clause. Ask these questions:

- Is insurance included in the quote, or is it optional and extra?
- What value is insured? Declare the true value of your goods.
- Who issues the policy, and can you see the policy document?
- How do you claim, and how long does it take?

For high-value items, consider arranging cover yourself and carrying the most precious things with you.

## What should the invoice show?

Ask for a proper invoice. It should show the mover's business name and address, the services, the amount and the invoice date. Do not rely on a figure a mover quotes verbally; check it on the invoice.

An invoice also helps if your employer reimburses relocation costs. If you are a bank employee on transfer, ask your HR team whether your movers must be on the Indian Banks' Association's approved list. Being on any list is not a guarantee of service.

## How much should you pay upfront?

Pay a small booking advance, and pay the balance on delivery, after you have checked the goods. Pay to a business account, by bank transfer or UPI, and get a receipt. Be wary of anyone who wants the full amount in cash before loading.

## What are the red flags?

- **A quote with no survey**, or a price that is far below the others
- **No company address or landline**, only a mobile number
- **An unmarked truck**, or a different company's name on the truck
- **Pressure to pay everything upfront**, or only in cash
- **No written terms, inventory or insurance details**
- **A new, higher price on moving day** for "extra" items that were on the survey
- **Reluctance to give references** or online reviews you can verify

Look the company up online, read recent reviews and ask a neighbour or a society office which movers have worked in your area. The same care you use against [rental scams](/blog/rental-scams-pune) applies here.

## What should you do if something is damaged or missing?

1. **Check the goods on arrival**, against the inventory, before the movers leave.
2. **Note damage on the delivery receipt** and photograph it.
3. **Do not sign "all received in good condition"** until you have checked.
4. **Write to the mover quickly**, with photos, and claim on the insurance, if any.
5. **If it is not resolved, complain to the National Consumer Helpline** on 1915 or through consumerhelpline.gov.in.
