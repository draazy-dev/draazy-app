### Rules for outreach

- Pitch one person at a time with a personal opening line. Never put several journalists in the To or Cc line.
- Do not offer money, free listings or gifts for coverage. If an outlet offers "sponsored" coverage, it must be labelled as such.
- Do not send attachments cold. Put the facts in the body and link to the page.
- Follow up once, after a week, then stop.
- Keep a log: outlet, person, date, reply. Do not re-pitch the same story.

### How to find better targets yourself

Search these phrases in Google (use India results) and look for **independent** bloggers, personal-finance writers, city newsletters and YouTube channels rather than competitors:

- `NoBroker alternatives` · `NoBroker vs MagicBricks vs Housing` · `best apps to rent a flat without a broker India`
- `flats for rent in Pune without broker` · `how to find a flat in Pune` · `moving to Pune checklist`
- `best apps to find flatmates in Pune` · `Pune relocation guide` · `Pune IT professionals where to stay`
- Reddit and Quora threads on the same questions often link to the roundups people actually read.

Also look at: relocation blogs, HR/relocation vendors' "moving to Pune" pages, college and corporate-parks resource pages, and local newsletters. A "resource" link on a useful page (for example your locality guides) is often easier to get than a place in a ranked list.

### Rules for listicle outreach

- Only ask for inclusion when Draazy is **live** and the claim in the email is true that day. A pre-launch "please add us" is wasted.
- Do not pay for a "sponsored" ranking and present it as editorial. Do not ask for a link in exchange for a link swap scheme.
- Offer something concrete: a factual one-line description, the page to link, and a screenshot they may use.
