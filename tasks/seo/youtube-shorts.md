## Ground rules

- **Facts come from the published guides.** Re-check any rupee figure or rule against the linked guide before you record. Fees change by government notification.
- Show real screens, real documents or plain text cards. No actors playing "tenants" with scripted testimonials. No invented numbers, no "thousands of users".
- Say what is true about Draazy: pre-launch (change when live), broker-free, a person at Draazy reviews every listing, staff check owner identity through online KYC, owner's number stays private behind a contact gate. Never say "OTP-verified owners".
- Titles are keyword-first, at most 70 characters (counts below were checked). Shorts show the first line of the description, so put the guide link in the first two lines.
- Chapters: YouTube chapters apply to longer videos, not Shorts. Timestamps in each script are for your editing only. If you ever cut a long-form version, reuse them as chapters (`0:00 Intro`, and so on, starting at 0:00, at least three, each at least 10 seconds).
- Add the standard footer to every description (see the template at the end).
- Spoken language: simple English or Hinglish. Keep captions on. Do not read a law section number aloud unless it helps.
